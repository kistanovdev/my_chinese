Your main speaking bottleneck is assembling familiar words into sentences: time and place before the verb, choosing the right verb, and finishing comparisons. You successfully used **有时候忙**, **不是每天都忙**, and **如果你找到新工作，你的工资可以涨很多** after prompting. Those are useful models to make automatic.

These ten priorities focus on your speech, treating Speaker 0 as you. Pauses and self-corrections are not counted as separate mistakes.

1. **Put time and place before the action.**  
   “他们起床在十点钟” → **他们十点钟起床。**  
   “如果你工作在美国” → **如果你在美国工作……**  
   “他学习在中国” → **他在中国学习。**  
   Pattern: **谁＋时间／在什么地方＋做什么**.

2. **Use 上节课／这节课／下节课 for lesson sequence.**  
   You repeatedly used “下课” or “上课” when explaining the previous lesson.  
   **上节课我们学了这部分。这节课我想继续学。**  
   上节课 = last lesson; 下节课 = next lesson. 上课 = attend/have class; 下课 = class ends. Your English explanation makes this a clear vocabulary mix-up.

3. **Describe frequency with 有时候 and 不是……都…….**  
   “可以忙……不每天” → **我的工作有时候忙，但不是每天都忙。**  
   可以 usually expresses permission or possibility; it does not mean “sometimes.”  
   **不是每天都忙** = not busy every day; **每天都不忙** = not busy on any day.

4. **Compare with A 比 B＋adjective.**  
   “需要照顾比去工作很难” → **照顾两个孩子比上班难。**  
   “他的工资比我很高，很大” → **他的工资比我的工资高很多。**  
   Use **高／低** for salary. **高很多／低很多** means much higher/lower; 太低 means “too low.” Compare salary with salary.

5. **Use 虽然……但是…… and 对……不满意.**  
   Your intended meaning was dissatisfaction with salary, while liking your work. The teacher briefly changed that meaning; you corrected it.  
   **虽然我对工资有点不满意，但是我不想换工作。**  
   **有点不满意** = a little dissatisfied; **换工作** = change jobs.  
   “我不满意我的工资” is understandable; **我对我的工资不满意** is a useful standard pattern.

6. **Distinguish 找 from 找到.**  
   “我不想去找到新公司” → **我不想找新工作。**  
   **找** = look for; **找到** = successfully find.  
   **现在找工作不容易。**  
   Your later sentence **如果你找到新工作，你的工资可以涨很多** was correct: the condition assumes a successful search.

7. **Use 都 before the verb; distinguish 比较 from 比.**  
   “我喜欢喝都” → **茶和咖啡我都喜欢喝。**  
   **我比较喜欢喝茶。** = I prefer tea in this context.  
   **这份工作比较忙。** = This job is fairly busy.  
   比较 before an adjective does not always mean an explicit comparison.

8. **Use 用＋tool＋action, and learn verbs as short chunks.**  
   “我每天做这个APP” → **我每天用这个APP复习汉字。**  
   “我的妈妈学习我” → **我妈妈教我用筷子。**  
   Keep these distinctions: **学习** learn; **教** teach; **复习** review; **记住** remember successfully; **记不住** cannot retain.  
   **复习** does not itself mean “memorize.”

9. **Use 觉得／看起来 for your impression.**  
   “很多汉字跟我一样” → **我觉得很多汉字很像。**  
   You meant that the characters resembled one another, rather than resembled you.  
   **一开始，我觉得“我”和“找”看起来很像。**  
   **一样** = the same; **很像** = very similar.

10. **Separate 没关系／无所谓 and 有意思／有意义.**  
   For “It’s okay / no problem”: **没关系。**  
   For “I don’t mind / either is fine”: **我无所谓。**  
   “我没关系” is not the usual standalone response for that meaning.  
   For your naming discussion: **我想要一个有意义的中文名字。**  
   **有意思** usually means interesting; **有意义** means meaningful. To ask about literal meaning, use **这个字是什么意思？**
