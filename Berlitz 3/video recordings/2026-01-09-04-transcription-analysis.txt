Your main speaking bottleneck is choosing the right verb and arranging the sentence while explaining a complex idea. You already use **我去过……、我没听过、我会……** correctly in several places. Practise those familiar patterns with small changes before adding more vocabulary.

The corrections below refer to Speaker 1. Without audio, some wording remains uncertain.

### Ten priorities

1. **有 = have; 要 = want/need**
   
   You repeatedly say “我要两个护照” and “我要两个女儿” where the context suggests possession.
   
   → **我有两个护照。我有两个女儿。**
   
   Likewise, “你要香港的护照？” → **你有香港的护照吗？**
   
   These are likely verb-choice mistakes, although transcription could account for individual occurrences.

2. **会 / 可以 / 能: skill, permission, circumstances**
   
   “我可以打羽毛球” is grammatical, but for a learned skill, prefer:
   
   → **我会打羽毛球，也会打一点乒乓球。**
   
   **我不会滑雪** means “I don’t know how to ski.” For your intended meaning of not skiing for a long time:
   
   → **我已经十二年没滑雪了。**
   
   “我不可以去运动” suggests prohibition. If circumstances prevent it: **我没时间运动。 / 我不能出去运动。**

3. **去过 versus 去了**
   
   **我去过秘鲁。** = I have been to Peru; an experience.
   
   When narrating the embassy visit:
   
   → **六年以前，我们在法国。我被抢劫了，所以去了俄罗斯大使馆。**
   
   Your **去过大使馆** is grammatical, but **去了** fits a particular event better. **他出国了** means he went abroad; whether he is still abroad depends on context.

4. **办签证；不用 versus 没有**
   
   “我不喜欢做签证” → **我不喜欢办签证，很麻烦。**
   
   “旅游没签证” mixes possession with necessity:
   
   - **没有签证** = don’t have a visa.
   - **不用办签证** = don’t need to get a visa.
   
   Useful question: **去这个国家旅游，需要签证吗？**  
   This is language practice, not verification of the visa claims in the lesson.

5. **给 / 帮: giving something and helping someone**
   
   In the passport discussion, “大使馆要护照” means the embassy wants/requires a passport, rather than provides one.
   
   Your intended question:
   
   → **如果护照丢了，大使馆能帮我办新护照吗？**
   
   Your explanation of the document:
   
   → **这个证件只能用来回俄罗斯。**
   
   Learn **帮我办……** as a whole phrase: “help me arrange/get……”.

6. **Time and frequency usually come before the action**
   
   “以前她去每个星期” → **以前她每个星期都去。**
   
   “我想去每个星期” → **我想每个星期都去练泰拳。**
   
   “在六年我没有钱” → **有六年时间，我没有钱。**
   
   Useful frame: **以前／现在／两年以后 + 谁 + 做什么**. Say **今年**, rather than “这个年”; **两年以后**, rather than “二年以后”.

7. **看比赛；如果……就……**
   
   “我喜欢看运动” → more naturally **我喜欢看体育比赛。**
   
   “我去比赛” can suggest participating. To attend as a spectator:
   
   → **我想去现场看比赛。**
   
   Your conditional idea:
   
   → **如果法国队或阿根廷队有比赛，我就去看。**
   
   **或／或者** presents alternatives; **和** joins things together.

8. **一起 versus 一边……一边……**
   
   For your treadmill explanation, “我工作和散步” → 
   
   **我一边工作，一边在跑步机上走路。**
   
   **一起** means “together”: **我想和女儿一起滑雪。**
   
   **也** means “also”: **我也喜欢滑雪。**
   
   Walking on a treadmill is **走路**; **散步** usually means taking a walk. Jogging is **慢跑**.

9. **想让 someone do something**
   
   “我想我的女儿弹钢琴” → **我想让我的女儿学弹钢琴。**
   
   **我想 + action**: what I want to do.  
   **我想让 + person + action**: what I want someone else to do.
   
   Keep these activity combinations together: **打羽毛球、踢足球、下国际象棋、练泰拳、弹钢琴、拉小提琴、吹笛子**.

10. **Numbers and measurements: omit 是 before 高／重**
    
    “我是高……180 centimeters” → **我身高一百八十厘米。**
    
    “我应该八十五斤体重” → **我的体重应该是八十五公斤。**  
    This corrects the sentence form, without endorsing a target weight.
    
    **公斤** = kilogram; **斤** = 0.5 kilogram in mainland usage.
    
    **十千** → **一万**; **一百万** = one million.  
    For US currency, say **一百六十美元**. **块** alone does not identify the currency.

### Transcript uncertainty and teacher wording

- **“我的超市……圣彼得堡”** probably contains a transcription error, possibly **城市**, but the original wording is uncertain. A safe location sentence is **圣彼得堡在俄罗斯的欧洲部分。** It does not establish your hometown.
- **“出去比赛”** probably represents **足球比赛**; **“滑鞋”** probably represents **滑雪**. Do not treat these as confirmed vocabulary mistakes.
- **“Safari……有脸”** is too unclear to reconstruct. There is no reliable basis for deciding which animal or word you meant.
- **“一次”** during your explanation of playing golf “with” someone may reflect confusion with **一起**. Use **和某人一起打高尔夫球**; **一次** means “once.”
- The teacher supplied **婆婆** for your mother’s mother. For broadly understood Mandarin, use **外婆／姥姥**. **婆婆** commonly means a woman’s mother-in-law, although regional usage differs.
- Other teacher wording to normalize: **普拉提**, **商人**, **想象一下** for “imagine,” and **减肥** = lose weight, rather than simply keep fit.
