### Ten useful corrections and structures

1. **Use verbs directly: 加班、照顾、散步、休息**  
   You repeatedly added 做: “我做加班”“我做照顾”“我做散步”“我们做 a break.”

   → **我有时候加班。** — I sometimes work overtime.  
   → **我照顾女儿。** — I take care of my daughter.  
   → **我带女儿去散步。** — I take my daughter out for a walk.  
   → **我们休息五分钟吧。** — Let’s take a five-minute break.

   These words already express actions. **我要加班** is also correct; 要 adds “need to / will.”

2. **Put time before the action**  
   “我吃在一点”“我吃一点” → **我下午一点吃午饭。**  
   “每个星期一天，两天……加班” → **我每个星期有一两天要加班。**  
   “从1月……2月，我做照顾” → **从一月到二月，我在家照顾孩子。**

   Useful frame: **谁 + 时间 + 动作**.  
   “我吃一点” normally means “I eat a little,” rather than “I eat at one.”

3. **Describe changing conditions with 有时候……有时候……**  
   “我的工作可以忙，可以轻松” → **我的工作有时候很忙，有时候比较轻松。**

   可以 usually expresses permission or possibility. 有时候 better describes your workload varying.  
   **简单** means simple; **轻松** means relaxed or undemanding. They are related, but not interchangeable.

4. **Use 大 with 压力; negate the description**  
   “压力很多” → **压力很大。**  
   “不，不压力” → **工作压力不大。**  
   If you mean no stress at all: **没有什么压力。**

   Useful lesson sentence: **因为工作压力太大了，所以他觉得很累。**  
   “Because the work pressure is too great, he feels tired.”

5. **Complete comparisons: A 比 B + adjective**  
   “工作比照顾很轻松” → **工作比照顾孩子轻松。**  
   To emphasize the difference: **工作比照顾孩子轻松得多。**

   Do not put 很 directly before the adjective in this basic 比 pattern.  
   From the reading: **现在的工资比以前高得多。** — My current salary is much higher than before.

6. **Distinguish 不用、不能、不想**  
   You needed clarification on “不用经常加班了.”

   - **不用加班。** — Don’t need to work overtime.
   - **不能加班。** — Cannot work overtime.
   - **不想加班。** — Don’t want to work overtime.

   **现在不用经常加班了。** means the situation has changed: frequent overtime is no longer necessary.  
   For “我的女儿不可以睡觉,” if you meant she could not fall asleep, say **她睡不着。** 不可以 sounds like she is not allowed to sleep.

7. **Use 的时候 and count frequency with 次**  
   “大学时候” → **上大学的时候**  
   “照顾时候” → **照顾女儿的时候**

   “每个睡觉……一个小时” → **她每次睡一个小时。**  
   Full pattern: **她每天睡三次，每次睡一个小时。**

   **每天** = every day; **每次** = each time; **三个小时** = three hours; **三次** = three times.

8. **Build job introductions with 是、在、做**  
   “我，软件工程师” → **我是软件工程师。**  
   “我工作客服” → **我做过客服。**  
   Your more specific meaning: **上大学的时候，我做过电话技术支持。**

   Useful frame: **我在 + 公司 + 做 + 职业。**  
   **我在苹果公司做软件工程师。**

   Keep the categories clear: **职业** = occupation; **行业** = industry. A **科技公司** is a type of company; **科技行业** is an industry.

9. **Place 我/你 inside the description: 我最喜欢的……**  
   “我的喜欢的肉” → **我喜欢的肉**  
   “你的最喜欢的肉什么？” → **你最喜欢的肉是什么？**

   You correctly reached: **我最喜欢的肉是牛肉。**

   “我很喜欢吃牛排店” → **我很喜欢去牛排店吃牛排。**  
   You eat the food; you go to the shop.

10. **Separate experience, events, and learning results**  
    **我听过别人这样说。** — I have heard people say it that way.  
    For the particular event you described: **昨天我听到有人说“APP”。**

    **我学了新东西** and **我学到了新东西** are both correct. 学到 emphasizes gaining something from learning; it does **not** guarantee complete mastery.

    Also keep **还不错** as a whole phrase: “pretty good / not bad,” rather than literally “not wrong.” **马马虎虎** means “so-so” here and is less positive.

- Written **他/她** cannot establish a spoken mistake: both are pronounced *tā*.

For pronunciation, remember **出差 chūchāi** versus **差不多 chàbuduō**. 出差 means traveling for work, rather than simply leaving home to work.
