### Ten priorities

1. **Put 在… and 跟… before the verb.**  
   You repeatedly said “工作在苹果” and “工作跟他.”  
   → 很多朋友想**在苹果公司工作**。  
   → 我可以**跟他一起工作**。  
   → 我们**在家工作**。  
   Pattern: **人 + 在哪里／跟谁 + 做什么**. Working from home is 在家工作, rather than 从家工作.

2. **Put clock times before the action.**  
   “我每天我起床在六点，七点。”  
   → 我每天**六七点起床**。  
   六七点 means approximately six or seven; use 六点 if you mean exactly six.

3. **Separate “ago,” “later,” and “for this long.”**  
   “我五年毕业。”  
   → 我**五年前毕业了**。— I graduated five years ago.  
   → 我**毕业五年了**。— It has been five years since graduation.  
   → 我2014年来美国，阿姨**三年以后**才来。  
   For ongoing work: **我在苹果公司工作八年了，做软件工程师四年了。** Your later “毕业五年了” was correct.

4. **Use 过 for past experience.**  
   “大学的时候我做了两个工作。”  
   → 我上大学的时候**做过两份工作**：送披萨和在图书馆工作。  
   **份** counts jobs; **过** highlights experience.  
   → 我**没用过**猎头公司。  
   “没看过猎头公司” does not clearly express “I haven’t used a recruitment agency.”

5. **Distinguish hearing from understanding.**  
   “我不可以听／不能听” during connection problems:  
   → 我**听不到你的声音**。— No sound reaches me.  
   → 我**听不清楚**。— The sound is unclear.  
   → 我**没听懂**。— I heard it but didn’t understand.  
   “我听过聪明” → 我**听到了“聪明”这个词**。  
   听过 means “have heard before.” For “很难听,” say **这段录音很难听懂**; 很难听 alone usually means unpleasant to hear.

6. **Build a small set of job-search phrases.**  
   “网上打广告” is understandable; for posting a vacancy:  
   → 公司**在网上发布招聘广告**。  
   → 我想**找一份新工作**。  
   → 我想**应聘这个职位**。  
   → 我要**更新简历**，然后把简历**发给公司**。  
   公司招聘人 = the company recruits; 人应聘职位 = someone applies for a position. A company recruiter is usually **招聘人员**, while **猎头** is a headhunter.

7. **Use a verb for “lie,” and 给 for the recipient of a call.**  
   “很多人谎言在他的简历。”  
   → 很多人**在简历里说谎**。  
   谎言 is a noun; 说谎 is the action.  
   “他打电话跟你” → 他**给你打电话**。  
   To introduce the conversation: **他给你打电话，跟你说：“……”**

8. **Finish comparisons and give a clear reason.**  
   “软件的面试比做工作难。”  
   → 我觉得**软件工程师的面试比实际工作更难**。  
   Your 比…难 structure was already on the right track; 更 is optional.  
   In the candidate exercise:  
   → 我觉得**小王更合适，因为他有三年的工作经验**。  
   Use **我觉得** for an opinion; 我想小王 is incomplete here.

9. **Practice 虽然…但是… with your cooperation example.**  
   “我可以工作跟你，虽然我不喜欢你。”  
   → **虽然我不喜欢你，但是我可以跟你一起工作。**  
   A more tactful version for an interview:  
   → **虽然我们意见不同，但是我能跟他一起工作。**  
   The second version is an alternative phrasing, not a claim about your actual situation.

10. **Describe abilities and habits naturally.**  
   “我学习很快” → 我**学得很快**。  
   “我看我的手机很多” → 我**经常看手机**／我**看手机的时间太多了**。  
   “我只有学士” → 我只有**学士学位**／我是**本科毕业的**。  
   **我没有耐性** is valid; **我不太有耐心** is another common choice.  
   **特长** means a particular skill; **优点** means a positive quality. Other people can share your 特长. **好学（hào xué）** means eager to learn, rather than academically successful.
