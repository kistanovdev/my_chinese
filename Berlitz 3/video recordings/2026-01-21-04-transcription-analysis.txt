Your biggest opportunity is to make a few familiar patterns automatic. You successfully corrected yourself with **我刚睡醒、我没听见手机闹钟、我上过西班牙文课**. Focus on producing these complete patterns without rebuilding them word by word.

I treat Speaker 0 as you and Speaker 1 as the teacher. Without audio, transcription judgments remain tentative; pauses and unfinished attempts are not automatically grammar mistakes.

1. **Put time and place before the action.**  
   “我睡觉在一点” → **我一点睡觉。**  
   “我到了美国在……2014” → **我2014年到了美国。**  
   “现在我工作……苹果公司” → **现在我在苹果公司工作。**  
   Pattern: **我＋时间＋在某地＋做什么。**

2. **上节课 / 下节课 / 下课**  
   You repeatedly used “下课” when apparently referring to the previous lesson. The teacher’s response supports this correction:  
   **上节课我们学了第十课，这节课我们学第十一课。**  
   **上节课** = last lesson; **下节课** = next lesson; **下课** = finish class.  
   Use **上课、学第十一课**, rather than relying on 做.

3. **刚 means “just recently”; 只 means “only.”**  
   “我只睡醒” → **我刚睡醒。** — I just woke up.  
   **我只睡了六个小时。** — I only slept six hours.  
   Your question here revealed a real English-to-Chinese ambiguity, which you then resolved correctly.

4. **会 / 能 / 可以 express different kinds of ability.**  
   **我会说西班牙语。** — learned skill  
   **我现在能说话。** — current ability  
   **我可以问一个问题吗？** — permission  
   Your switching between 不会、不可以 and 不能 suggests this distinction needs practice. For a blocked nose, **我的鼻子不通气** is more precise than **我不能呼吸**, which means “I can’t breathe.”

5. **没听见 / 听不见 / 听不懂**  
   “我不听了我的手机” → **我没听见手机闹钟响。**  
   **没听见** = didn’t hear it on that occasion.  
   **听不见** = can’t hear it; **听不懂** = can hear it but can’t understand it.  
   Similarly, **说不了话** means “unable to speak”; 不了 cannot stand alone before 话. Here, 了 in 不了 is pronounced *liǎo*.

6. **Use the right unit—and put age differences after 大 / 小.**  
   “五个天” → **五天**; **六个小时** is correct.  
   “三十年” when describing age → **三十岁**.  
   “我的太太比我一年大” → **我的太太比我大一岁。**  
   Pattern: **A＋比＋B＋大/小＋几岁。**

7. **Choose a specific verb instead of 做 / 去 / 有.**  
   “你做了高考？” → **你参加过高考吗？**  
   “五月三十号我要……HSK三考试” → **五月三十号我要参加HSK三级考试。**  
   “我去过西班牙文课” → **我上过西班牙语课。**  
   Useful combinations: **参加考试、上课、读本科、读硕士**.  
   For education, **我在……读的本科** is clearer than 我有……, which can sound like possession.

8. **Ask for feedback or vocabulary with a complete question.**  
   “你觉得什么？” → **你觉得……怎么样？**  
   **你觉得我的中文说得怎么样？**  
   **你觉得我的发音怎么样？**  
   **这个词怎么用？能再举一个例子吗？**  
   **“Engineering”用中文怎么说？**  
   发音 = pronunciation; 声调 = Mandarin tones; 工程 = engineering; 工程师 = engineer.

9. **很难＋verb; 找到 emphasizes success.**  
   “我们这一代……难工作” → **我们这一代很难找到工作。**  
   **找工作** = look for work; **找到工作** = successfully find work.  
   **很难找到** expresses difficulty more accurately than the absolute **不能找到**.  
   Also, **老师，这个很难** addresses the teacher; **老师很难** describes the teacher. For “strict,” use **老师很严格**.

10. **上网 is an action; 在网上 gives the location.**  
    Your confusion between 网上 and 上网 was explicit:  
    **我们这一代经常上网。**  
    **我在网上看了很多关于三十岁的笑话。**  
    Prefer **经常上网** or **花很多时间上网** to the teacher’s awkward 上很多网.
