Your biggest opportunity is to turn familiar words into complete, reusable sentences. You often communicated your meaning successfully, but repeatedly struggled with verb choice, word order, and time expressions.

I treat Speaker 1 as you **outside the recorded dialogues and reading exercises**. Without audio, transcription errors and pronunciation mistakes cannot always be separated.

### Ten priorities

1. **Use specific verbs instead of 买 for every transaction.**  
   You repeatedly said “买酒店,” “买六百美元,” and “买一个司机.”

   - **订酒店／订房间** — book a hotel / room
   - **住酒店** — stay in a hotel
   - **买机票／订机票** — buy / book a flight ticket
   - **每年付六百美元的年费。** — Pay an annual fee of $600.  
     This appears to be your intended meaning in the credit-card discussion.
   - **包车，有司机。／租车，自己开。** — Hire a car with a driver / rent a car and drive yourself.

   You correctly changed “买一个酒店” to **订一个酒店** later in the lesson.

2. **Put 跟 + person before the activity.**  
   “我睡觉跟我的大女儿” → **我跟大女儿一起睡，我太太跟小女儿一起睡。**  
   “我旅游跟我的女儿” → **我跟女儿一起去旅游。**

   For “travel by myself”: **我一个人去旅游。**  
   The repeated “旅游司机” is unclear transcription or an unsuccessful attempt at **自己**; your English clarification establishes the intended meaning.

3. **Use 比……更喜欢…… for preferences.**  
   Your “我喜欢高铁，比……坐飞机” was unfinished.

   **比起坐飞机，我更喜欢坐高铁。**  
   “Compared with flying, I prefer taking high-speed trains.”

   Keep this separate from adjective comparisons:  
   **坐高铁比坐飞机舒服。** — Taking high-speed trains is more comfortable than flying.

4. **Use 从……到…… for routes; 在…… for location.**  
   “中国旅游的时候” → **在中国旅游的时候。**  
   Your Beijing–Shanghai sentence was understandable; smoother:  
   **我想从北京坐高铁到上海。**

   “我在加州住在……” → **我住在加州。**  
   “我怕开车……美国的外面” → **我怕在美国以外的地方开车。**

5. **Separate 以前, 以后, and 第二天.**  
   You sometimes used **以后** while describing the old airport.

   **以前机场很小，现在机场很大。**  
   “The airport used to be small; now it is large.”

   For a general travel routine:  
   **第一天先去酒店休息，第二天再出去玩。**

   **明天** means tomorrow relative to today; **第二天** means the next day in a story or itinerary. **休息** was the appropriate word you were trying to retrieve for “rest.”

6. **Say 两天、三天; distinguish each person from the total.**  
   “两个天、三个天” → **两天、三天**.  
   Hours do take 个: **两个小时**.

   Your later clarification resolves the ticket-price misunderstanding:  
   **五个人一共三千美元，不是每个人三千美元。**

   **每个人** = each person; **一共** = altogether.  
   Ask about price with **多少钱**: **去香港的机票多少钱？**

7. **Replace 跟我 with 对我来说 for “for me.”**  
   You used “跟我，很贵” and similar expressions several times.

   **对我来说，太贵了。** — It is too expensive for me.  
   **如果不经常旅游，付这个年费就不划算。** — If you do not travel often, paying this annual fee is not good value.

   Useful distinction: **便宜** = inexpensive; **划算** = good value; **值得** = worthwhile.

8. **Use 看…… for “it depends.”**  
   A practical answer to the baggage question:

   **这要看航空公司，也要看你买的机票。**  
   “It depends on the airline and the ticket you buy.”

   The teacher’s “是否 / 是 who” likely represents **视乎**, a regional expression. **是否** means “whether,” so do not memorize it as “depends.”

   Airport words worth keeping distinct:

   - **办理登机手续** — check in
   - **过安检** — go through security
   - **护照检查** — passport control
   - **托运行李** — check baggage

   Your passport story concerned **护照检查**, which you were trying to distinguish from 安检.

9. **Use 会／不会 for skills; 没……过 for experience.**  
   “我不可以说……以中文的” → **我不会用中文说。**  
   “我不知道说……” → **我不知道怎么说。**

   For your driving experience:  
   **我没在美国以外的地方开过车。**

   Useful classroom rescue phrases:  
   **请再说一遍。** — Please say it again.  
   **你能说慢一点吗？** — Could you speak more slowly?  
   **这个词是什么意思？** — What does this word mean?

10. **Learn 就 in complete phrases, alongside 完 and 了.**  
    In the reading, you interpreted 就 as “quickly.” It does not inherently mean that.

    - **我早就想去了。** — I have wanted to go for a long time.  
      You successfully produced this yourself.
    - **吃了你就知道了。** — Once you try it, you will know.
    - **票已经卖完了。** — The tickets are already sold out.  
      **完** marks completion.
    - **现在已经没有这个问题了。** — We no longer have this problem.  
      Here **了** marks a changed situation.

### Transcription cautions

- **“夜幕” → 岳母** is very likely a transcription error: you had just learned and repeated 岳母.
- **“新蛋节”** in the teacher’s speech probably means **圣诞节**.
- Repeated **地铁** when describing intercity sleeper trains could be a word-choice error or transcription problem. The intended term is **火车／卧铺火车**; you clearly self-corrected 地铁 to 高铁 earlier.
- Garbled fragments such as **“跟我平……”** are too uncertain to reconstruct confidently.
- The fluent recorded dialogues are lesson material, not evidence of your spontaneous speaking.
