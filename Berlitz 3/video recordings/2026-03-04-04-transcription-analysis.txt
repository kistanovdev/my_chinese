Your main speaking priority is **choosing the right verb and putting time expressions before it**. You often knew the topic vocabulary but struggled to assemble a complete sentence. Practise short sentences first, then add details.

These are the ten most useful corrections and structures from the lesson. Quoted fragments omit hesitation and repetition.

1. **坐飞机／坐经济舱 — use 坐 for transport and cabin class**

   “我……飞机是经济舱” → **我坐经济舱。**  
   “每个飞机有很多经济舱” → **飞机上有很多经济舱座位。**

   坐 = travel by; 座位 = seat. A plane has economy seats, rather than “many economy cabins.” The usual aircraft measure word is **架**：一架飞机。

2. **没＋verb＋过 — describe experiences you haven’t had**

   “我还没有去头等舱，也还没有去商务舱。” →  
   **我还没坐过头等舱，也没坐过商务舱。**

   **过** marks experience: **我坐过高铁。／我去过日本。**  
   Your **你去过日本吗？** was already correct.

3. **订酒店／住酒店 — book versus stay**

   Your repeated “买五星级酒店” needs a different verb:

   - Booking: **我们订了一家五星级酒店。**
   - Staying: **我住过五星级酒店。**

   **买酒店** means buying the hotel itself. Your Japan story can begin: **我们去日本旅游的时候，订了一家五星级酒店。**

4. **Time before the verb; verb before its duration**

   “他们打扫在早上” → **他们早上打扫房间。**  
   “你想睡觉几点？” → **你想几点睡觉？**  
   “我们可以做五分钟休息……” → **我们可以休息五分钟吗？**

   Pattern: **谁＋什么时候＋做什么**。  
   For duration: **休息五分钟／住三个星期／等两个小时**。

   For “make the bed,” use **铺床**; **做床** suggests constructing a bed.

5. **Verb＋完／好 — finished versus ready**

   From the reading: **经济舱的票卖完了。／酒店订好了。**

   - **完** = finish: **饭吃完了。**
   - **好** = successfully completed or ready: **酒店订好了。**

   **完 wán** and **玩 wán** sound identical but have different meanings. **玩游戏** means play games; **卖完了** means sold out.

6. **从……到……＋transport＋要＋duration**

   Your travel plan becomes:  
   **明年去中国旅游的时候，我想坐高铁从北京到上海。**

   Useful question: **从北京到上海坐高铁要多长时间？**

   **从北京去上海** is also correct. For travel time, use **要**: **要三个小时。**  
   The lesson’s **直达** means direct; it does not necessarily mean no intermediate stops.

7. **两＋measure word; choose the word for the object**

   “二个小时” → **两个小时**  
   “一个飞机票” → **一张飞机票**  
   “一个托运行李” → **一件托运行李**

   Useful chunks: **两个小时／两张票／一件行李／一个箱子**。  
   **二百美元** is correct; **两百美元** is also common.

8. **付钱；托运要另外付钱 — express costs clearly**

   “我买二百美元” → **我付了两百美元。**

   Given your explanation about baggage fees, “航空公司没有托运行李” probably meant:  
   **机票不包括免费托运行李。**  
   A simpler spoken version: **机票很便宜，但是托运行李要另外付钱。**

   **买** = buy something; **付** = pay. Also distinguish **买一个新箱子** from **付托运行李的钱**. Your later “买新的托运” is unclear about which you meant.

9. **靠窗／靠走廊；坐在…… — seat location**

   “我喜欢去走廊的位子” → **我喜欢坐靠走廊的位子。**

   You later clearly reversed the meanings:

   - **靠窗** = by the window; **可以看外面**
   - **靠走廊** = by the aisle

   Useful request: **我想换一个靠窗的座位，可以吗？**  
   For your preference near the front: **我喜欢坐在前面，因为下飞机的时候不用等那么久。**

10. **Ask for meaning; distinguish 不用 from 不能**

   “我不知道它航站楼” → **我不知道“航站楼”是什么意思。**  
   “我不知道说这个中文” → **我不知道这个用中文怎么说。**

   These sentences help you keep speaking when vocabulary is missing.

   In the security discussion:
   - **不用拿出来** = don’t need to take it out
   - **不能拿出来** = cannot / are not allowed to take it out

   Your intended opinion about security’s effectiveness was unclear. If you meant “security checks aren’t useful,” say **我觉得安检没有用。** That differs from **不用安检** (“no security check is needed”).
