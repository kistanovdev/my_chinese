Your biggest speaking gains will come from **comparisons, choosing 会／可以／能, and putting phrases in a reliable order**. You already produced good sentences such as “我们收藏冰箱贴,” “我喜欢学习中文,” and “我的家有五个卧室.”

I treat Speaker 1 as you during conversation. Lines from the recorded dialogues and your reading aloud are lesson material, not evidence of spontaneous speaking. Without audio, transcription judgments remain tentative.

### Ten priorities

1. **会 = know how; 可以 = permission or possibility**

   You used “不知道下棋” and “不可以说” when discussing skills.

   - **我会下国际象棋，但是不会下中国象棋。**
   - **我会说西班牙语。**
   - **这里可以下棋吗？** — Are we allowed to play chess here?

   Your later explanation suggests “不知道下棋” may have meant you did not recognize the word or the pictured game. It does **not** establish that you cannot play chess.

2. **买不起 = cannot afford**

   When discussing expensive housing, “不可以买” sounds like buying is prohibited. “不会买” can mean you will not buy, or do not know how.

   - **太贵了，我买不起。**
   - **如果我有钱，我想在日本买一套房子。**

   Use 买不起 only when cost is the reason; some of your unfinished statements leave the reason unclear.

3. **Adjectives usually do not need 是**

   Recurring examples: “不是难,” “不是贵,” and “不是好.”

   - **不难。／很容易。** — Not difficult / easy.
   - **不贵。／不是很贵。** — Not expensive / not very expensive.
   - **不太好。** — Not very good.

   **不是很贵** is natural, so you do not need to avoid every expression containing 不是.

4. **Comparisons: A 比 B + adjective**

   This recurred with karaoke, dancing, watches, and living conditions.

   - “不是好比韩国……中国” → **这里的卡拉OK没有韩国或中国的那么好。**
   - “比 Rolex 不贵” → **Tudor 比 Rolex 便宜。**
   - For your dance comparison: **Bachata 比探戈和萨尔萨容易。**

   For “she lives better than I did,” make the comparison explicit:  
   **她现在住的房子比我小时候住的大。**

5. **Learn hobbies with their verbs**

   “我做下棋” → **我下棋。**  
   “我很喜欢欧洲下棋” → **我很喜欢下国际象棋。**

   Useful combinations: **下棋、玩桌游、打牌、唱歌、跳舞、骑马、爬山、收藏冰箱贴**.

   **我每天都在网上下棋。**

   These are established combinations; the teacher’s “use your hands → 打” shortcut does not work for every activity.

6. **Put 跟 + person before the action**

   “我喜欢爬山……跟朋友” → **我喜欢跟朋友一起爬山。**

   Your final question becomes:  
   **你跟女儿说广东话还是普通话？**

   广东话 is also Chinese, so **普通话** makes the intended contrast clearer.

7. **Past experience and duration**

   Your spontaneous “我去过 Las Vegas” was correct.

   - **你去过澳门吗？／我没去过。** — Experience: 过.
   - “我住在公寓……十二年” → **我以前在俄罗斯的公寓里住了十二年。**
   - If still living somewhere: **我在这里住了一年了。**

   For your Spanish-learning starting point:  
   **我从2013年开始学西班牙语。**  
   This does not assume you studied continuously ever since.

8. **除了……还／也…… = in addition to**

   This was the lesson’s main structure:

   - **除了电脑，我的房间里还有很多书。**
   - **除了下棋，我还喜欢学习中文。**

   Here 除了 means **“besides / in addition to,”** rather than exclusion. Your “除了电脑，我的房间里还有什么？” was a correctly formed question; the practice goal was to answer it in a full sentence.

9. **没有钱 versus 免费; 不必要 versus 不一定**

   These express different ideas:

   - **我没有钱。** — I have no money.
   - **这个网站是免费的。／这个网站不要钱。** — The website is free.
   - “不必要你有钱” → **会骑马不一定说明你有钱。** — Being able to ride does not necessarily mean you have money.

   In the horse discussion, your later reference to one horse suggests you meant its upkeep: **养一匹马很贵。** “持续骑马” means continuing to ride, which is a different idea.

10. **Repair phrases for when a word will not come**

   “不听” does not express a failed listening attempt.

   - **我没听清楚。** — I did not hear it clearly.
   - **我没听懂。** — I heard it but did not understand.
   - **请再说一遍。**
   - **这个词用中文怎么说？**
   - “你可以……写” → **你可以写下来吗？**

   These short phrases will help you keep speaking through a vocabulary gap.

### Transcript uncertainty and teacher corrections

- **Likely transcription errors:** the teacher’s “绿音” probably means **录音**; “喀什克” likely means **哈萨克**. Your “在你图” may mean **在地图上**. Do not memorize the garbled forms.
- **Unclear fragments:** “南州,” “东部的贴,” and “我去上了秋天” do not support a confident correction. “以后我赚钱打 poker” also leaves the intended time unclear; I would not assume past gambling income or a future plan.
- **Clear recurring learner patterns:** “做下棋,” “比 Rolex 不贵,” and skill-related “不可以说” are useful correction targets.
- **Teacher guidance to adjust:** standard Mandarin uses **一匹马**; apartment area is measured in **平方米**, not simply 米. **刻板印象** means stereotype; **休闲时间** means leisure time. 香山 is a place name, not slang.
