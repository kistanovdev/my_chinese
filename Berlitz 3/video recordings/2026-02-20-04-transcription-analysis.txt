Your biggest opportunity is to make familiar words easier to assemble spontaneously: **time/place + action**, **不 versus 没**, and a few reusable patterns. You successfully used **我没听过、我吃过、我觉得** and, after explanation, **一边……一边……**.

The transcript labels both you and the teacher “Speaker 0,” so attribution is sometimes uncertain. The corrections below focus on turns that appear to be yours; pauses and unfinished restarts are not automatically grammar mistakes.

### Ten priorities

1. **不是每天都…… — not every day**  
   Around 03:13: “我每天不……没有……开会。”  
   → **我不是每天都开会。**  
   This means you have meetings on some days. **我每天都不开会** means you have no meetings on any day.

2. **Put time and place before the action**  
   Around 29:43: “我们能吃饭在公司。”  
   → **我们可以在公司吃饭。**  
   Around 30:51: “我们的开会在……”  
   → **我们下午一点半开会。**  
   Useful frame: **谁 + 什么时候 + 在哪里 + 做什么**.  
   For work, **上班** is usually more natural than **去工作**. A company dining hall is normally **食堂**; the transcribed “餐堂” may be a word mix-up or recognition error.

3. **不 / 没（有）/ 没……过 — habit, past event, experience**  
   Your discussion of “我工作没有迟到” shows a real point of confusion:
   - **我上班一般不迟到。** — I generally don’t arrive late for work.
   - **我今天没迟到。** — I wasn’t late today.
   - **我上班从来没迟到过。** — I’ve never been late for work.

   **没有** negates an action here; it does not mean “does not have.” These are meaning contrasts, not conclusions about your attendance history.

4. **Ask about an unfamiliar word directly**  
   Around 06:09: “我不知道更改。”  
   → **我不知道“更改”是什么意思。**  
   Around 07:26: “我忘了迟到。”  
   → **我忘了“迟到”是什么意思。**  
   Also useful: **请再说一遍。** / **请说慢一点。**  
   For scheduling, **改时间** is an easy alternative to **更改**: **可以把会议改到星期一吗？**

5. **去不了 + 如果…… — inability and conditions**  
   Around 06:50: “如果我不可以去开会，我的同事不做……不改。”  
   If you meant the meeting stays at the same time:  
   → **如果我去不了，他们也不会改开会的时间。**  
   **去不了** means “can’t go”; **不可以去** can suggest “not allowed to go.” Your teacher interpreted this as “they won’t invite you,” but the transcript does not establish that this was your meaning.

6. **来得及 / 来不及；提前…… — enough time / in advance**  
   Around 16:35: “九点半是来得及。”  
   → **九点半出门来得及。**  
   Around 17:38: “你应当去三个小时。”  
   → **你应该提前三个小时到机场。**  
   **提前三个小时** means three hours before the relevant event. This is language practice using the lesson’s scenario, not a recommendation about airport arrival times.

7. **比……多 / 没有……那么好 — comparisons**  
   Around 22:32: “美国有比哈萨克有很多饭馆。”  
   → **美国的饭馆比哈萨克斯坦的多。**  
   Around 18:53: “我们的机场比……不好。”  
   → **我们的机场没有中国或香港的机场那么好。**  
   **还是** usually introduces alternatives in a question; **或／或者** suits alternatives in statements. Also, objects do not normally take **们**: **机场**, not **机场们**.

8. **Adjectives usually don’t need 是；use 都 for “all”**  
   Around 30:27 and 01:04:44: “每个东西是贵。”  
   → **这里的东西都很贵。**  
   Or: **每样东西都很贵。**  
   Related restaurant language: **味道不错，但是有点贵。**  
   **有点贵** = a little expensive; **太贵了** = too expensive.

9. **最喜欢吃……；每两天……一次**  
   Around 26:16: “是我最喜欢的吃饭。”  
   → **我最喜欢吃这种面。**  
   **吃饭** is an action, not the noun “food.”  
   Around 26:42: “每二天。”  
   → **每两天吃一次。** — Eat it once every two days.  
   Around 01:09: “再次火锅。”  
   → **我想再吃一次火锅。** — I want to eat hot pot again.  
   **再 + action + 一次** is especially reusable.

10. **小时候；一起 + action；住了 + duration**  
    Around 44:00: “孩子的时候……我们玩一起很多。”  
    → **小时候，我们经常一起玩。**  
    Around 45:45: “我在俄罗斯住在十二年，去哈萨克四年。”  
    → **我在俄罗斯住了十二年，后来在哈萨克斯坦住了四年。**  
    For “haven’t lived there for many years”: **我已经很多年没在俄罗斯住了。**  
    Avoid **我不是在俄罗斯很多年**; **不是** does not express this meaning.

### Useful lesson phrases to keep

- **快十二点了。／快下课了。** — It’s almost noon. / Class is almost over. Usually say **两点**, rather than **二点**.
- **我现在不饿，十点左右会饿。** — I’m not hungry now; I’ll be hungry around ten.
- **中午一起吃饭吧，我们边吃边聊。** — Let’s have lunch and chat while we eat. **一边……一边……** describes simultaneous actions.
- **今天我请客。／我们AA吧。／那我就不客气了。** — My treat. / Let’s split the bill. / Then I’ll gladly accept.
- **这家餐馆的菜很正宗。** — This restaurant’s food is authentic. **正宗** is more precise than **真的** for cuisine.
