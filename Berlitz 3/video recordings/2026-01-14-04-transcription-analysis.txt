These corrections concern Speaker 1. Without audio, pronunciation mistakes and transcription errors cannot always be separated.

1. **你在哪个国家？— asking about location**  
   You said “你在什么国家里？” and “你在国家哪里？”  
   → **你在哪个国家？你在哪个城市？**  
   Use **哪个 + noun** for “which.” **你在哪里？** asks “Where are you?” Country names normally do not need **里**.

2. **……多长时间了？— duration up to now**  
   “我在美国十二年。” → **我在美国十二年了。**  
   **你学中文多长时间了？我学中文一年了。**  
   Final **了** signals that the duration reaches the present. You subsequently used this correctly.

3. **我听说…… / 我觉得……— reporting versus giving an opinion**  
   “我听瑞国很贵 / 我听了……” → **我听说瑞士很贵。**  
   “我认为你喜欢这个视频。” → **我觉得你会喜欢这个视频。**  
   **听说** = have heard that; **觉得** = think/feel; **会** expresses the prediction “will.” Repeated **瑞国** likely reflects difficulty recalling **瑞士**, although transcription may contribute.

4. **还是、和、又……又……、但是— choosing the right connector**  
   “你住在还是学习？” → **你在瑞士工作还是学习？**  
   “中文是容易和困难。” → **中文有的地方容易，有的地方难。**  
   Use **还是** for alternatives in questions, **和** mainly between nouns, and **又……又……** for two qualities/actions: **又工作又学习**. For contrast: **瑞士很漂亮，但是生活很贵。**

5. **来 / 去 + 过— direction and experience**  
   Your **我想去瑞士** was correct from your location in the US. The teacher in Switzerland can ask **你想来瑞士吗？**  
   From your location: **你来过美国吗？我去过欧洲。**  
   **过** marks experience. Negative: **我还没去过瑞士。** Your earlier “你去过美国” could be a spoken question with question intonation; **吗** makes it explicit.

6. **看得懂 / 听得懂 / 听出来— different kinds of understanding**  
   “我可以看” → **我看得懂汉字。**  
   “我听……你的口音……” → **我听出来你有北方口音。**  
   **看得懂** = understand what you read; **听得懂** = understand speech; **听出来** = identify something by listening. **认识汉字** means recognize characters: **我认识四百个汉字。**

7. **对……来说— “for someone”**  
   Your attempts with “英文很难……给中国人 / 跟中国人” needed this structure:  
   → **对我来说，说中文很难。**  
   **跟 / 和某人说话** means speak *with* someone; **对某人来说** introduces their perspective. The teacher’s “每个中国人都想说英文” is an unsupported generalization, not a necessary correction of your idea.

8. **学、练习、参加、通过、问— useful verb combinations**  
   “做每个课” → **我想学第二册的每一课。**  
   “做HSK” → **参加HSK二级考试**; passing → **通过HSK二级考试**.  
   “做很多请问” → **我在中文课上问很多问题。**  
   **请问** politely introduces a question; it is not the noun “question.” Also use **练习听力 / 练习口语 / 练习手写** rather than “用听 / 用说.”

9. **如果……就…… / 只有 / 只— conditions and limited time**  
   Your long explanation becomes easier in short sentences:  
   **如果每天只有一个小时学中文，就先练习听、说、读。**  
   **如果不练习手写，手写汉字就会很难。**  
   **只有一个小时** = have only one hour; **只练习听力** = practice only listening. This was a hypothetical discussion; it does not establish your actual daily study time.

10. **下课以后 + request; A和B不一样— practical word order**  
   Repeated “中文课下” → **下课以后，你可以把笔记发给我吗？**  
   Your final comparison was already correct:  
   **你的口音和我的香港老师的口音不一样。**  
   Repeating **口音** makes clear that you are comparing accents. For your later “关于瑞士我不知道很多,” use **我不太了解瑞士。**
