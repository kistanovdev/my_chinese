Your main practice target is **retrieving complete sentence patterns while speaking**. You often knew the vocabulary, then needed help with word order or finishing the structure. You also corrected yourself successfully—for example, 一首歌、五百个汉字 and 我把其他同事介绍给你.

These are the ten highest-value targets.

1. **Describe your job: 我是…… / 我是做……的**

   You repeatedly said “我是做软件工程” and once “我不做言语治疗师.”

   Natural versions:

   - **我是软件工程师。** — job title
   - **我是做软件工程的。** — type of work
   - **我不是言语治疗师。 / 我不做言语治疗。**

   Keep **是 + job title** separate from **做 + work/activity**. Your original 我是软件工程师 was already correct.

2. **Put the workplace before 工作: 在……工作**

   “她Cupertino中学工作” → **她在库珀蒂诺的一所中学工作。**

   For the future meaning you were trying to express:
   **孩子长大以后，她会在库珀蒂诺的一所中学工作。**

   Ask: **你在哪家公司工作？**
   Answer: **我在苹果公司工作。**

   The transcript leaves her current work situation unclear; “家里工作” alone does not establish whether she works remotely or is caring for your child.

3. **Put duration after the verb: 工作四年了**

   “我在苹果公司四年工作” → **我在苹果公司工作四年了。**

   “我住在美国十二年” → **我在美国住了十二年了。**

   These describe a situation continuing up to now. For recent learning:
   **最近我学了很多中文。**

   Both 学 and 学习 are valid; 学 is often more conversational.

4. **Compare: 比……好 / 没有……好 / 比……差**

   “美国的科技行业比中国科技行业不好” →
   **我觉得美国的科技行业没有中国的科技行业好。**

   Alternatively:
   **我觉得美国的科技行业比中国的科技行业差。**

   Pattern:

   - **A比B好一点。** — A is a little better.
   - **A没有B好。** — A is not as good.
   - **A比B差一点。** — A is a little worse.

   In “没有中国的科技行业的好,” remove the final 的. These corrections preserve your expressed opinion; they do not verify the economic claim.

5. **Distinguish frequency from ability: 多 vs 好**

   You clarified that you speak English **more often** than Russian:

   **现在我说英语比说俄语多得多。**

   That differs from:
   **我的英语比俄语好。** — My English is better than my Russian.

   Also, “英文说很好” → **我英语说得很好。**
   Use **verb + 得 + description** to describe how well someone does something.

6. **Use 如果……就…… for hypothetical choices**

   Your later sentence was good:
   **如果我不在科技行业工作，我就去金融行业工作。**

   For a hypothetical preference, this is especially clear:
   **如果我不在科技行业工作，我就想去金融行业工作。**

   The final 了 is unnecessary for this basic hypothetical. Learn the full chunks **在……行业工作**, rather than trying to build everything around 做.

7. **Separate 会、能、可以—and ability from understanding**

   Your claim that “我们不说我可以开车” was too absolute.

   - **我会开车。** — I have learned the skill.
   - **我今天不能开车。** — circumstances prevent it.
   - **我可以开车吗？** — permission or whether it is an option.

   These meanings overlap; 能 does not simply mean “right now.”

   For reading:

   - **我认识五百个汉字。** — recognize the characters
   - **我会读这些字。** — know how to pronounce/read them
   - **这句话我看得懂。** — understand the written sentence
   - **这段录音我听不懂。** — cannot understand the recording

8. **Introduce someone: 给你介绍 / 介绍给你**

   You repeatedly mixed 跟 and 给:

   “我跟你介绍一个新同事” → **我给你介绍一个新同事。**

   “我把其他同事介绍跟给你” →
   **我把其他同事介绍给你。**

   Memorize both complete patterns:

   - **我给你介绍一下，这是小周。**
   - **我把小周介绍给你。**

   Your final use of the 把 pattern was correct.

9. **认识 people; 知道 information; 听说过 names**

   “我知道一个” in the discussion about someone you personally knew →
   **我认识一个这样的人。**

   Natural questions:

   - **你认识说话不清楚的人吗？**
   - **你知道这个词是什么意思吗？**
   - **你听说过Drake吗？**

   Also: “你是第一中国人” →
   **你是我认识的第一个信耶稣的中国人。**

   The reusable structure is **我认识的 + 第一个 + ……人**.

10. **Use natural word combinations**

   - “做钱在美国” → **在美国挣钱**
   - “我们做在美国” → **我们在美国生产这些东西**
   - **新同事 / 老朋友 / 旧衣服** — useful combinations you practiced
   - “没有新同事” as a translation of *No New Friends* → **没有新朋友**

   老朋友 usually means a longtime friend, not an elderly friend. The teacher’s “旧 for things, 老 for people” is a useful shortcut, but not an absolute rule.
