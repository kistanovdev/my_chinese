3. Meanings to correct from the lesson

- 欢迎 means “welcome”; it does not mean a person. 各位 is a polite way to address a group: “everyone” or “ladies and gentlemen.” 位 is a polite measure word for people.
- 参观 means to visit or tour a place to look around and learn about it. Asking questions is optional. 参加 means to take part in an activity: 参观工厂, but 参加会议.
- Airspace is 空域, not 宇宙飞船. 宇宙飞船 means spacecraft. “使用俄罗斯的空域” expresses the idea you were discussing.
- You can say “我想买一辆理想汽车” or “我想买一部苹果手机。” You do not automatically need 品牌 when a brand name also has another meaning.
- 外国的产品 is valid Chinese. 进口产品 specifically means products brought into a country from abroad.
- 拳头产品 means a company’s flagship or leading competitive product. It does not simply mean any good-quality product. 畅销 means selling well.
- 销 and 银 share the component 钅; 销 does not contain 银. 油 means oil and has 氵 on the left. 抽 is a different character with 扌 on the left.

4. Small vocabulary list

Learn these six actively this week.

- 产品 chǎnpǐn - product
- 生产 shēngchǎn - to produce or manufacture
- 功能 gōngnéng - function or feature
- 设计 shèjì - design; broader than appearance alone
- 材料 cáiliào - material
- 木头 mùtou - wood

For reading recognition only, keep 拳头产品 quántou chǎnpǐn, 畅销 chàngxiāo and 销售额 xiāoshòu’é. 销售额 is the money earned from sales, not the number of items sold.

5. Useful phrases

- 请再说一次。Qǐng zài shuō yí cì. - Please say it again.
- 这个词是什么意思？Zhège cí shì shénme yìsi? - What does this word mean?
- 这个字怎么读？Zhège zì zěnme dú? - How do you read this character?
- 我知道意思，但是忘了怎么读。Wǒ zhīdào yìsi, dànshì wàng le zěnme dú. - I know the meaning, but I’ve forgotten how to read it.

6. Sentence structures and drills

Spend about eight minutes a day. Read each model aloud, then cover it and say the variations. Finish with one true sentence about yourself.

- 再 + verb + 一次: do something again.
  我再读一次。I’ll read it again.
  请再说一次。
  我再听一次。
  Drill: Ask the teacher to say something again, then say you will read it again.

- 有 + something + 要 + verb: have something to do.
  我有很多工作要做。I have a lot of work to do.
  我有一个问题要问。
  我有一封邮件要写。
  Drill: Say you have work to do, a question to ask, and an email to write.

- 是在 + place + 生产的: say where something was made.
  这个玩具是在中国生产的。This toy was made in China.
  这些手机是在中国生产的。
  这辆车是在美国生产的。
  Drill: Answer “这个玩具是在哪里生产的？” with a complete sentence.

- 是用 + material + 做的: say what something is made of.
  这张桌子是用木头做的。This table is made of wood.
  这个杯子是用玻璃做的。
  这把椅子是用塑料做的。
  Drill: Describe three objects around you. Use 木头, 玻璃 or 塑料.

- 除了……，还……: besides one thing, also another.
  我们公司除了生产手机，还生产电脑。Our company makes computers as well as phones.
  手机除了能打电话，还能拍照。
  我今天除了看YouTube，还看了Instagram。
  Drill: Say two things your company makes, then two things your phone can do.

- 对我来说，……更重要: say what matters more to you.
  对我来说，功能更重要。For me, features matter more.
  对我来说，设计更重要。
  有时候设计更重要，有时候功能更重要。
  Drill: Answer “功能和设计，哪个更重要？” Then add one short reason using 因为.

Start with the first three drills until you can say them smoothly. Then add the remaining three.
