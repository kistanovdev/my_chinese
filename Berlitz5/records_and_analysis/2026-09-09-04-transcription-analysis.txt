1. What to focus on

You can explain real situations and ask useful questions, but building sentences quickly is harder than understanding explanations. Focus on short sentences about your work and daily life before combining several ideas. This transcript alone cannot establish your level, especially because reading unfamiliar characters adds difficulty.

Your main priorities are:
- Saying “must” with 要 or 必须.
- Using whole phrases such as 参加会议 and 忘了上课.
- Recognizing words you understood orally but struggled to read: 灵活、拒绝、福利、沟通、精神.

2. Important sentence corrections

- 00:00:10: 我在九点起床，今天不是工作。
Better: 我九点起床，以为今天不用工作。
I got up at nine and thought I didn’t have to work today.
Use 不用工作 for “don’t have to work.” 以为 describes what you thought was true.

- 00:00:19: 我忘了也我，我也有中文课。
Better: 我忘了今天还有中文课。
I forgot I also had Chinese class today.
Put the thing you forgot after 忘了.

- 00:01:13: 我不能错中文课。
Better: 我不能错过中文课。
I can’t miss Chinese class.
Learn 错过 as a complete word.

- 00:02:49: 我可以读没有拼音，你觉得什么？
Better: 我想试试不看拼音读课文，你觉得怎么样？
I want to try reading the text without looking at pinyin. What do you think?
你觉得怎么样 asks for an opinion.

- 00:07:06: 我一定去开会们。每个开会我一定去。
Better: 我必须参加每次会议。
I must attend every meeting.
开会 is a verb; 会议 is a noun. Do not add 们 to either. 一定要 expresses obligation; 一定 alone often expresses certainty.

- 00:07:38: 我要很多工作，不能去这个开会。
Better: 我有很多工作要做，不能参加这个会议。
I have a lot of work to do and can’t attend this meeting.

- 00:12:24: 十五百分之……
Better, if you meant a 15% pay increase: 如果工资增加百分之十五，我就可以每周工作六天。
If my pay increases by 15%, I can work six days a week.
The order is 百分之十五.

- 00:17:34: 我最喜欢的例如是……
Better: 我最喜欢的例子是……
My favorite example is…
例子 is the noun “example.” 例如 introduces examples: 例如，猪和狗。

- 00:30:24: 还要团队精神。
Better: 还要有团队精神。
You also need team spirit.
Keep 有 when saying someone needs to have a quality.

- 00:57:51: 这是我十年以后。
Better: 这是十年前的我。
This is me ten years ago.
以前 means before; 以后 means after.

- 01:00:12: 她很小了。
If you mean your sister is younger: 她比我小。
She is younger than me.
To give the difference: 她比我小五岁。She is five years younger than me.

These corrections focus on clear patterns. Some isolated words, such as 免费 where 面试 was expected, could be transcription errors; the text alone cannot confirm pronunciation.

3. Vocabulary to learn first

Keep this week’s new vocabulary to these eight items. Learn each with its phrase.

- 错过 cuòguò — miss: 错过中文课
- 灵活 línghuó — flexible: 工作时间很灵活
- 会议 huìyì — meeting: 参加会议
- 工资 gōngzī — pay/salary: 工资增加了
- 福利 fúlì — employee benefits: 福利很好
- 接受 jiēshòu — accept: 接受这份工作
- 拒绝 jùjué — reject/decline: 拒绝这份工作
- 沟通 gōutōng — communicate: 跟同事沟通

For reading, pay particular attention to 灵活 versus 生活, and 接受 versus 接手. 接手 means “take over,” rather than simply “accept.”

4. Useful phrases

- 我忘了今天还有中文课。— I forgot I had Chinese class today.
- 我有很多工作要做。— I have a lot of work to do.
- 我必须参加会议。— I must attend the meeting.
- 请把拼音遮住。— Please cover the pinyin.
- 这个字怎么读？— How do you pronounce this character?
- 我给你看一张照片。— I’ll show you a photo.

5. Sentence structures and drills

Spend most of your study time here. Read each model aloud, then cover it and produce the variations. Aim for a complete sentence before trying to speak faster.

- 必须 / 一定要 + verb: obligation.
Model: 我必须参加会议。
Variations: 我必须完成工作。/ 我一定要上中文课。/ 我今天一定要参加会议。
Then say: “I can work in the evening, but I must attend meetings.”
Answer: 我可以晚上工作，但是我必须参加会议。

- 有 + something + 要 + verb: something you need to do.
Model: 我有很多工作要做。
Variations: 我有一个会议要参加。/ 我有一些资料要看。
Then say: “I have a lot of work to do today.”
Answer: 我今天有很多工作要做。

- 如果……，就……: condition and result.
Model: 如果明天不用工作，我就九点起床。
Variations: 如果工资增加，我就接受这份工作。/ 如果明天有中文课，我就早点起床。
Then say: “If I have a meeting, I’ll attend.”
Answer: 如果我有会议，我就参加。

- 虽然……，但是……: contrast.
Model: 虽然工资很高，但是工作时间不灵活。
Variations: 虽然福利很好，但是我不想周末工作。/ 虽然今天是假期，但是我还有中文课。
Then say: “Although I have a lot of work, I still want to attend Chinese class.”
Answer: 虽然我有很多工作要做，但是我还是想上中文课。

- 不但……，而且……: add a second point.
Model: 这份工作不但工资高，而且福利也很好。
Variation: 他不但工作能力强，而且跟同事的关系也很好。
也 is optional in these examples.
Then say: “This job has both flexible hours and good benefits.”
Answer: 这份工作不但工作时间灵活，而且福利也很好。

Practice the first two patterns every day, then choose one of the remaining three. Five to ten minutes is enough.

6. A few teacher explanations to correct

- 贝 means shell, not turtle shell. The money association comes from shells used as currency.
- 部首 is the component used to classify a character in a dictionary. It is not defined by being at the top or bottom; 偏旁 is a broader term for character components.
- 本科 refers to undergraduate education; 学士 is a bachelor’s degree; 硕士 is a master’s degree. 博士后 is a research position or stage after a doctorate, not a higher degree.
- 猪 and 狗 share 犭; 马 does not. The shared component in 蛋 and 强 is 虫, pronounced chóng, rather than 重.
- For your work meeting, 每日例会 is a useful expression. You can explain: 我们叫它“站会”，但是我们不一定站着开会。We call it a stand-up, but we don’t necessarily stand during it.
