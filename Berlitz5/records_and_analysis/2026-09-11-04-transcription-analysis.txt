1. What to focus on

- Your main difficulty in this lesson was turning an idea into a complete sentence and recognizing familiar words without pinyin. Practice short, useful sentences before adding more vocabulary.
- Prioritize explaining your learning needs, asking for help, and talking about meetings.
- Most speech is labeled Speaker 0, so I cannot reliably separate every teacher sentence from yours. Some apparent mistakes may also be transcription errors.

2. Corrections worth practicing

- “一点不错” → “我的听力还可以。” My listening is okay. If you mean it needs improvement: “我的听力不太好。” 一点不错 is unnatural for either meaning.
- “我学习中……汉字……一点” → “我学过一点汉字。” I have studied a little Chinese writing. For an ongoing activity: “我正在学汉字。”
- “我明白狗、猫、猪……” → “我认识‘狗’‘猫’‘猪’这几个字。” I recognize these characters. Use 认识 for recognizing characters; 明白 for understanding an explanation.
- “第一是目 / 眼是六的” → “第一个是‘目’。第六个是‘眼’。” Use 第 + number + 个 when identifying items.
- “你一点去看，对？” → “你的意思是没有仔细看，对吗？” You mean looking without examining carefully, right?
- “我想去浴室” → “我想去一下洗手间。” 浴室 means a room for bathing; 洗手间 fits your request during class.
- “我们可以读黄的” → “我们可以读黄色的部分吗？” Can we read the yellow section? Adding 部分 makes the reference clear.
- “我要一个开会，我已经去” → “我等一下要开会，得走了。” I have a meeting shortly and need to go.

- Reading corrections: 加入, not 加人; 讨论, not 对论; 还有意见吗, not 还没意见吗. These are character recognition issues rather than necessarily grammar mistakes.
- 结束 means “to end”; 介绍 means “to introduce.” Practice them together: “会议结束了。请介绍一下自己。”

3. Vocabulary: learn these six first

- 讨论 - to discuss. 我们讨论一下这个问题。
- 总结 - to summarize. 请总结一下。
- 计划 - plan. 这个计划很好。
- 意见 - opinion or suggestion. 你有什么意见？
- 认识 - to recognize or know. 我不认识这个字。
- 结束 - to end. 我们的课几点结束？

4. Useful phrases

- 这个字怎么读？ - How do you read this character?
- 这个词是什么意思？ - What does this word mean?
- 请把拼音遮起来。 - Please cover the pinyin.
- 我觉得他说得有道理。 - I think what he said makes sense.

5. Sentence structures and drills

- Practice these aloud. Read the model, change the indicated part, then make one sentence without looking. Spend most of your study time here.

- 有点 + adjective: describes a slight degree, often something undesirable. Verb + 一点 + noun: describes a small amount.
- 我有点累。 - I am a little tired.
- 我有点紧张。 - I am a little nervous.
- 我学过一点汉字。 - I have studied a little Chinese writing.
- Drill: Say “I am a little busy,” then “I understand a little Chinese.” Answers: 我有点忙。／我懂一点中文。

- Subject + time + 在 + place + action.
- 我今天在家学中文。
- 我们星期一上午在公司开会。
- 我和朋友星期六在公园散步。
- Drill: Change 今天 to 明天, 在家 to 在公司, and 学中文 to 开会. Keep the order stable.

- Verb + 一下: makes an action sound brief or softens a request.
- 请解释一下这个词。
- 我们讨论一下这个计划吧。
- 请总结一下今天的内容。
- Drill: Make requests using 看、读、介绍. Models: 请看一下。／请读一下。／请介绍一下自己。

- 我觉得……: state your understanding or opinion.
- 我觉得这个字很难。
- 我觉得这个计划很好。
- 我觉得我的听力还需要练习。
- Drill: Complete “我觉得……” with one sentence about your class, one about your listening, and one about a plan.

- 如果……，就……: condition and result.
- 如果我不认识这个字，就问老师。
- 如果你有时间，我们就讨论一下。
- 如果大家都没有意见，就这么办吧。
- Drill: Say “If I don’t understand, I’ll ask the teacher.” Answer: 如果我不明白，就问老师。

6. A manageable practice routine

- Spend five minutes daily on two drill patterns. Repeat each model, change one detail, then say your own version.
- Finish with these three sentences from memory: “我学过一点汉字。”“这个字怎么读？”“我等一下要开会，得走了。”
- Leave idioms such as 目中无人 and 走马看花 for later. Complete everyday sentences will help you more immediately.
