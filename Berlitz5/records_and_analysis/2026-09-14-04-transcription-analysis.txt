1. What to focus on

Your main difficulty is turning an idea into a complete sentence quickly. You often know what you want to say, but struggle with word order, time expressions, and choosing the right verb. Recognizing characters is a separate difficulty: reading mistakes do not always mean you misunderstand the grammar.

I would prioritize short sentences about your work and daily life before longer technical explanations. This transcript alone cannot establish your Berlitz level, and I cannot assess your tones reliably without audio.

2. Vocabulary and phrases to learn

Learn these six items as whole phrases.

- 解决问题 — jiějué wèntí — solve a problem
- 商量一下 — shāngliang yíxià — discuss something together
- 紧急会议 — jǐnjí huìyì — emergency meeting
- 消息 — xiāoxi — news or an update
- 通知你 — tōngzhī nǐ — notify you
- 需要帮忙 — xūyào bāngmáng — need help

Keep these distinctions clear:

- 消息 = news or an update; 信息 = information; 销售 = sales or selling.
- 退钱 = refund money; 退货 = return goods.
- 销售 does not mean shopping or a discount sale. 买东西 = shop/buy things; 打折 = offer a discount.
- 项目 = project; 话题 = topic; 目标 = goal.

3. Important sentence corrections

These corrections focus on your own speech. Some fragments may reflect transcription errors or difficulty reading the textbook.

- 00:24 我们一点学习。
Better: 我们学了一点儿。
We studied a little.
Put 一点儿 after the verb when it expresses the amount studied.

- 07:00 你的吃饭给我拉肚子。
Better: 我在你们饭店吃饭以后，拉肚子了。
I had diarrhea after eating at your restaurant.
This expresses the sequence without needing a complicated sentence about causation.

- 13:45 一个人可以有很多人跟随。
Better: 一个人可以有很多粉丝。
A person can have many followers.
On social media, 关注 means follow an account; 粉丝 means followers or fans.

- 14:44 这个地方是干净吗？
Better: 这个地方干净吗？
Is this place clean?
A simple adjective predicate usually does not need 是.

- 17:10 我要很大拉肚子。
Better: 后来我拉肚子了，很严重。
Later, I had severe diarrhea.
Use 严重 for severity; 我要 expresses wanting or intending to do something.

- 30:00 一百电脑。
Better: 一百台电脑。
One hundred computers.
Use 台 to count computers: 两台电脑、三台电脑.

- 34:31 十五百分之折扣。
Better: 百分之十五的折扣。
A fifteen percent discount.
You can also say 打八五折: pay 85 percent of the original price.

- 36:11 一百美元跌了。
Better: 价格跌了一百美元。
The price fell by one hundred dollars.
Order: subject + 涨了/跌了 + amount.

- 42:50 没有真的例会，我有视频的例会。
Better: 我们的例会都是视频会议。
Our regular meetings are all video meetings.
例会 describes a regular meeting; 视频会议 describes its format.

- 01:22 比我他们知道多。
Better: 他们比我知道得多。
They know more than I do.
Order: A + 比 + B + verb + 得 + comparison.

4. Sentence structures and drills

Spend most of your practice time here. Read each model aloud, cover it, and say it again from memory. Then change one detail.

- 一……就…… = as soon as
我一有消息，就通知你。
As soon as I have an update, I will notify you.
会议一结束，我就回家。
As soon as the meeting ends, I will go home.
系统一出问题，我们就开会。
As soon as the system has a problem, we hold a meeting.
Drill: Say “As soon as class ends, I will go to work.”
Answer: 中文课一结束，我就去工作。

- 如果……就…… = if……then……
如果系统有问题，我们就检查一下。
If the system has a problem, we check it.
如果他们需要帮忙，就给我们打电话。
If they need help, they call us.
如果软件不能用，我们就先解决问题。
If the software does not work, we solve the problem first.
Drill: Say “If the customer is unhappy, we discuss it.”
Answer: 如果客户不满意，我们就商量一下。

- ……以后，…… = after……
吃饭以后，我拉肚子了。
After eating, I had diarrhea.
开会以后，我给你打电话。
After the meeting, I will call you.
下课以后，我去看女儿。
After class, I will go see my daughter.
Drill: Replace 开会以后 with “after getting home.”
Answer: 回家以后，我给你打电话。

- ……是为了…… = the purpose is to……
我们开会是为了解决问题。
We hold meetings to solve problems.
今天开会是为了讨论计划。
Today's meeting is to discuss the plan.
我们检查系统是为了发现问题。
We check the system to find problems.
Drill: Say “Today's meeting is to exchange information.”
Answer: 今天开会是为了交流信息。

5. Corrections to explanations in class

- Many years ago is 很多年前, not 很多年以后. Four years ago is 四年前; four years later is 四年后.
- In standard Mandarin, 约时间 is yuē shíjiān. Practice yuē rather than yāo.
- Profit margin is 利润率. Your translation was correct; 利润的幅度 is not the usual equivalent.
- 一百万 = one million; 一亿 = one hundred million; 十亿 = one billion.

6. A manageable practice routine

- Practice one drill group per day for five minutes.
- Say each sentence three times, then change the subject or action.
- Finish by answering: 今天为什么开会？ Use two short sentences: 系统有问题。我们开会是为了解决问题。
- For character practice, start with just 消息、销售、信息. Recognize each whole word and say its meaning before reading a longer passage.
