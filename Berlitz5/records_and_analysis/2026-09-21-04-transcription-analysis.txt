1. What to focus on

- You can explain complex ideas, but you often get stuck choosing a verb or arranging a sentence. Your priority should be short, complete sentences that you can say without translating each word.
- Focus on expressing opinions, describing experience, and asking clear questions. Leave 集思广益 and 垄断 for later.
- This analysis uses only this transcript. Hesitations and possible transcription errors are not treated as definite mistakes.

2. Vocabulary: learn these 8 items

- 准备 zhǔnbèi: to prepare. It does not mean “suggest.” 我需要准备明天的会议。I need to prepare for tomorrow’s meeting.
- 联系 liánxì: to contact. 我会联系客户。I will contact the customer.
- 经验 jīngyàn: experience. 他很有经验。He is very experienced.
- 观点 guāndiǎn: viewpoint. 我不同意他的观点。I disagree with his viewpoint.
- 说服 shuōfú: to persuade. 我说服了他。I persuaded him.
- 人手 rénshǒu: staff; available workers. 我们人手不够。We are understaffed.
- 招聘 zhāopìn: to recruit or hire. 我们需要招聘两个人。We need to hire two people.
- 促销 cùxiāo: sales promotion. 我们下个月要搞促销。We will run a promotion next month. 销售 means selling or sales; a promotion can involve more than a price reduction.

3. Phrases: learn these as complete chunks

- 我再读一遍。Wǒ zài dú yí biàn. I’ll read it again.
- 这个词是什么意思？Zhège cí shì shénme yìsi? What does this word mean?
- 这两个字看起来很像。Zhè liǎng ge zì kàn qǐlái hěn xiàng. These two characters look similar.
- 我们该怎么办？Wǒmen gāi zěnme bàn? What should we do?
- 我马上要开会了。Wǒ mǎshàng yào kāihuì le. I have a meeting shortly.

5. A few explanations from class need adjusting

- 原因 and 理由 are not divided into past and future. 原因 usually means a cause; 理由 usually means a reason or justification. 明天不能来的原因是什么？and 他昨天没来的理由是什么？are both possible.
- 对手 does not have 朋友 as a strict opposite. A competitor can also be a friend. 竞争 is competition or competing; 竞争对手 is a competitor.
- 可行 kěxíng means feasible: possible to carry out in practice. It does not simply mean “the boss approves.”
- 接受 means “accept”; 同意 means “agree.” 我接受你的决定 does not necessarily mean 我同意你的观点。
- 连……都 often corresponds to “even.” 连老板都不知道。Even the boss doesn’t know.
- “Opinionated” is not simply 有很多观点. A neutral description is 他很有主见。A critical description is 他很固执，不容易接受别人的意见。

6. Sentence structures and drills: your main practice

- Practice two groups per day. Read each model aloud, then cover it and rebuild it using the prompts. Keep the sentences short.

- Drill 1: Subject + 有 + number + 年的 + experience.
  我老板有二十六年的工作经验。
  My boss has 26 years of work experience.
  Change: 我 / 三年; 她 / 十年; 这个老师 / 五年。
  Then contrast: 我今年二十九岁。我有三年的工作经验。

- Drill 2: 我认为……，因为……。
  我认为我们需要招聘两个人，因为人手不够。
  I think we need to hire two people because we are understaffed.
  Change: 需要准备 / 明天要开会。
  Change: 这个计划不可行 / 人手不够。
  Change: 这个手机很好 / 很方便。

- Drill 3: 我同意／不同意 + person + 的观点。
  我不同意他的观点，因为这个手机太贵了。
  I disagree with his viewpoint because this phone is too expensive.
  Change: 同意 / 老板 / 这个计划可行。
  Change: 不同意 / 同事 / 我们没有时间。
  Follow with: 我想说服他接受我的观点。

- Drill 4: 每 + measure word + noun + 都 + action.
  每场比赛我都看了。
  I watched every match.
  每个孩子都有手机。
  Every child has a phone.
  每次开会我都发言。
  I speak at every meeting.
  Change: 每个客户 / 联系了; 每个星期 / 上中文课。

- Drill 5: 看了 versus 看过 versus 在看.
  昨天你看了比赛吗？Did you watch the match yesterday?
  你看过世界杯比赛吗？Have you ever watched a World Cup match?
  我给你打电话的时候，你在看比赛吗？Were you watching the match when I called?
  Repeat with: 电影; 电视。
  在看 describes an action in progress. A past time reference makes it past; you do not need a separate Chinese past-tense form.

- Drill 6: 连 + emphasized item + 都 + predicate.
  我太忙了，连周末都要工作。
  I’m so busy that I even have to work on weekends.
  这个问题太难了，连老师都不知道答案。
  This question is so difficult that even the teacher doesn’t know the answer.
  我很喜欢足球，连女儿都知道。
  I like football so much that even my daughter knows.
  Build your own sentence with: 太贵了 / 老板 / 不想买。

7. A short speaking drill combining the lesson

- Say this without looking:
  我们团队人手不够。
  我认为我们应该招聘两个人。
  开会的时候，我会说出我的观点。
  如果老板不同意，我会解释原因。
  他有二十六年的工作经验，所以我也会听他的意见。

- Then change just three details: the staffing problem, your proposed solution, and the number of years of experience. Your goal is to say five complete sentences smoothly.
