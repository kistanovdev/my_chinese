1. What to focus on

- Your main difficulty is building complete sentences while speaking. You often know the topic but struggle with word order, choosing the verb, and finishing the sentence.
- Prioritize short sentences about your own experiences, interests, and work. You do not need to memorize all the political, financial, and space vocabulary from this lesson.
- Your sentence “如果你喜欢你的老板，是坏消息。如果你不喜欢你的老板，是好消息” was clear. Keep practicing this kind of simple, complete answer.
- This transcript alone is not enough to assign you a Berlitz level. It does show that basic sentence patterns need more automatic practice.

2. Main corrections

- 03:20: “我还没工作跟坏老板。”
Better: 我没跟不好的老板一起工作过。
Meaning: I have never worked with a bad boss.
Put 跟……一起 before 工作. Use 没……过 for something you have never experienced.

- 03:54: “你在英国多少年住在？”
Better: 你在英国住了多少年了？
Meaning: How many years have you lived in the UK?
Order: person + 在 + place + verb + duration.

- 05:26: “你在中国……工作了吗？”
For “Have you ever worked in China?”: 你在中国工作过吗？
工作了吗 asks whether someone has started working or whether an action happened, depending on context. 工作过吗 asks about experience.

- 10:49: “黑龙江很近俄罗斯。”
Better: 黑龙江离俄罗斯很近。
Meaning: Heilongjiang is close to Russia.
Use A 离 B 很近.

- 22:03: “我网上读报纸。”
Better: 我在网上看报纸。
Meaning: I read newspapers online.
在网上 comes before the action. Both 读报纸 and 看报纸 are possible.

- 29:30: “文章是……读文章有意思，我读。”
Better: 如果文章有意思，我就看。
Meaning: If the article is interesting, I read it.
Adjectives usually do not need 是: 文章有意思.

- 32:21: “我不喜欢的听广播。”
Better: 我不喜欢听广播。
If you mean sound quality: 我不喜欢听广播，因为音质不太好。
Do not insert 的 between 喜欢 and the activity.

- 55:36: “她是我的岁。”
Better: 她和我同岁。
Meaning: She is the same age as me.

- 59:49: “我跟 Tim Cook 有一个图片。”
Better: 我有一张和 Tim Cook 的合照。
Meaning: I have a photo of me with Tim Cook.
Use 一张照片. 合照 means a photo taken together.

- 01:22:00: “一个人可以国际象棋，比我好。”
Better: 我认识一个人，他下国际象棋下得比我好。
Meaning: I know someone who plays chess better than me.
Use 下棋 for playing chess and 得 to describe how well someone does an activity.

3. Vocabulary: learn these six first

- 网络 — wǎngluò — network / internet connection. 这里的网络很好。The internet connection here is good.
- 音质 — yīnzhì — sound quality. 音质很好。The sound quality is good.
- 字幕 — zìmù — subtitles. 这个视频有中文字幕。This video has Chinese subtitles.
- 博主 — bózhǔ — online content creator. 我喜欢这个博主的视频。I like this creator’s videos.
- 采访 — cǎifǎng — interview. 他接受了采访。He gave an interview.
- 毕业 — bìyè — graduate. 她从大学毕业了。She graduated from university.

4. Useful phrases

- 关于中国的视频 — guānyú Zhōngguó de shìpín — videos about China.
- 和我同岁 — hé wǒ tóngsuì — the same age as me.
- 对我来说很容易 — duì wǒ lái shuō hěn róngyì — easy for me. Use 对我来说, rather than 跟我, for “for me.”
- 看足球比赛 — kàn zúqiú bǐsài — watch football matches. 踢球 means play football.
- 接受采访 — jiēshòu cǎifǎng — be interviewed.

5. Sentence structures and drills

Practice these five groups first. Read each example aloud, then cover the Chinese and say it from the English. Finally, change one detail to make your own sentence.

- Experience: person + 没 + verb + 过
我没去过中国。I have never been to China.
我没跟不好的老板一起工作过。I have never worked with a bad boss.
我没看过这个视频。I have never watched this video.
Question drill: 你去过中国吗？Have you ever been to China?
Answer drill: 还没去过，明年我打算去。Not yet. I plan to go next year.
没看过 means “have never watched.” 还没看 means “haven’t watched yet.”

- Place before action: person + 在 + place + verb
我在网上看新闻。I read news online.
我在公司工作。I work at a company.
我在 YouTube 上看视频。I watch videos on YouTube.
Question drill: 你在哪儿看新闻？Where do you read the news?
Answer drill: 我在网上看新闻。I read the news online.

- Duration: person + 在 + place + verb + 了 + duration + 了
我在加州住了三年了。I have lived in California for three years.
他在英国住了二十多年了。He has lived in the UK for over twenty years.
Question drill: 你在这里住了多久了？How long have you lived here?
Answer drill: 我在这里住了两年了。I have lived here for two years.
Replace the place and duration with your real answer.

- Conditions: 如果……，就……
如果文章有意思，我就看。If the article is interesting, I read it.
如果我有时间，我就看比赛。If I have time, I watch the match.
如果视频有字幕，我就更容易看懂。If the video has subtitles, it is easier for me to understand.
Prompt drill: What do you do if you have time?
Answer: 如果我有时间，我就……。

- Comparisons: A + 比 + B + adjective; verb + 得 + 比……好
我比他高一点。I am a little taller than him.
这个视频比那个视频容易懂。This video is easier to understand than that one.
他下棋下得比我好。He plays chess better than me.
Question drill: 谁下棋下得更好？Who plays chess better?
Answer drill: 他下棋下得比我好。He plays chess better than me.

6. A few teacher corrections worth keeping

- 油管 means YouTube, the platform. For the person making videos, use 博主 or 视频创作者. On Bilibili, UP主 is also common.
- For a chess player, 棋手 is more precise than 玩家. A chess master is 大师, not 大老师.
- 影响 means “influence” or “effect.” For the amount of influence someone has, use 影响力: 他的影响力很大。
- Gagarin was the first person in space; he did not go to the Moon. The distinction to remember is 进入太空 versus 登上月球.
- Several words may have been misheard by the transcription system. Do not memorize 收读 as a word for rap; use 说唱.

7. Daily practice: eight minutes

- Two minutes: say the six vocabulary examples aloud.
- Four minutes: practice two drill groups, changing the person, place, or activity.
- Two minutes: answer these questions in complete sentences: 你在哪儿看新闻？你去过中国吗？如果文章有意思，你会看吗？
- Aim for one finished sentence before adding more detail.
