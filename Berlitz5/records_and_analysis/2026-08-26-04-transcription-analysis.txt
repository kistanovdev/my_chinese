1. What to focus on

- Your main difficulty in this lesson was building complete sentences during spontaneous conversation. Focus on short sentences about your schedule and work before learning more recruitment vocabulary.
- Your sentences “我没听过苹果公司请猎头” and “纽约比加州贵” were already good. Hesitation alone is not a grammar mistake.
- Some transcript fragments are unclear. The corrections below focus on sentences where your intended meaning is reasonably clear.

2. Useful corrections

- “天的时候” - Use 白天 for “during the day”: 白天我们没有时间。For “in the morning”: 早上我们没有时间。
- “我说我的太太” - 我跟我太太说，早上八点我有中文课。Use 跟某人说 for “tell someone.”
- “今天他不起床” - 今天她还没起床。Use 还没 for “hasn’t yet.” If you mean your daughter, write 她; 他 and 她 sound identical.
- “对我的来说……很一样” - 对我来说，学习和休息这两个词的发音很像。Use 对我来说, without 的. 很像 means “very similar”; 一样 means “the same.”
- “也两个助理” - 公司要招聘一个项目经理和两个助理。Use 和 to join nouns.
- “每年也有的招聘会” - 我的大学每年也有招聘会。No 的 is needed.
- “很多人想工作在苹果” - 很多人想在苹果公司工作。Put 在 + place before 工作.
- “伦敦比纽约一样” - 伦敦和纽约一样贵。Use 和……一样 for equality; 比 for differences.
- “你的电脑不用，我帮忙你” - 你的电脑不能用了，我帮你。不能用 means “doesn’t work”; 不用 means “don’t need to use.” Say 帮你 or 帮你的忙.
- “我四年……这个公司工作以后” - 我在这家公司工作了四年以后，想换工作。Put the duration after 工作了.
- “十点做中文课” - 我想把中文课改到上午十点。The phrase for taking a class is 上课.

One clarification about the teacher’s explanation:
- 为了 expresses purpose; 因为 expresses a reason. 为了招聘新员工，我们联系了猎头公司。Because the company needs staff: 因为公司缺人，所以我们要招聘新员工。

3. Vocabulary to learn

- 招聘 zhāopìn - recruit; the company recruits people.
- 应聘 yìngpìn - apply for a job; the applicant applies.
- 职位 zhíwèi - job position.
- 软件工程师 ruǎnjiàn gōngchéngshī - software engineer. 高级软件工程师 means senior software engineer; 工人 means worker.
- 技术支持 jìshù zhīchí - technical support.
- 至少 zhìshǎo - at least. 二十万美元 means US$200,000; 三十万美元 means US$300,000.

4. Phrases to learn as complete chunks

- 改到上午十点 gǎi dào shàngwǔ shí diǎn - reschedule to 10 a.m.
- 跟我太太说 gēn wǒ tàitai shuō - tell my wife.
- 联系猎头公司 liánxì liètóu gōngsī - contact a recruitment agency.
- 参加招聘会 cānjiā zhāopìnhuì - attend a job fair. 举办招聘会 means organize one.

5. Sentence structures and drills

Spend about ten minutes a day on these. Say each model aloud, change one detail, then say it without looking.

- Subject + time + 在 + place + verb.
  我每天在家工作。I work at home every day.
  我明天在家上中文课。I have Chinese class at home tomorrow.
  Drill: Say “My wife works at home in the morning.”
  Answer: 我太太早上在家工作。

- Subject + 还没 + verb.
  我的女儿还没起床。My daughter hasn’t got up yet.
  我还没联系老师。I haven’t contacted the teacher yet.
  Drill: Say “We haven’t found a new employee yet.”
  Answer: 我们还没找到新员工。

- 我想把 + event + 改到 + time.
  我想把中文课改到上午十点。I want to move Chinese class to 10 a.m.
  Drill: Change the time to tomorrow afternoon at three.
  Answer: 我想把中文课改到明天下午三点。

- Company + 招聘 + person; person + 应聘 + position.
  我们公司想招聘两个软件工程师。Our company wants to recruit two software engineers.
  我想应聘公司的另一个职位。I want to apply for another position at the company.
  Drill: Say “Our team wants to recruit a senior software engineer.”
  Answer: 我们团队想招聘一个高级软件工程师。

- 在 + place + verb + 了 + duration.
  我在苹果公司做了四年技术支持。I worked in technical support at Apple for four years.
  我在伦敦旅游了十天。I traveled in London for ten days.
  Drill: Say “I worked at this company for three years.”
  Answer: 我在这家公司工作了三年。

- A + 比 + B + adjective; A + 和 + B + 一样 + adjective.
  伦敦比我的城市贵。London is more expensive than my city.
  伦敦和纽约一样贵。London is as expensive as New York.
  Drill: Say “This position is harder than that position.”
  Answer: 这个职位比那个职位难。

6. Final speaking drill

Practice this short version of your work story. Once you can say it comfortably, change the company, duration, and job.

- 我上大学的时候，参加了一个招聘会。
- 后来，我在苹果公司做了四年技术支持。
- 做了四年以后，我想换工作。
- 一个亲戚跟我说：“你可以应聘公司内部的其他职位。”
- 我现在是软件工程师。
