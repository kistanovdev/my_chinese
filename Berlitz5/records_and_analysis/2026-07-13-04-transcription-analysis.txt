- 02:17 我不能冲浪。 If you mean you lack the skill: 我不会冲浪。 会 describes a learned skill; 能 describes ability or circumstances.

3. Vocabulary: learn these six first

- 气味 qìwèi - smell. 这种气味很重。 This smell is strong.
- 晕车 yùnchē - get carsick. 我坐在后排会晕车。 I get carsick in the back seat.
- 景点 jǐngdiǎn - tourist attraction. 北京有很多景点。 Beijing has many attractions. 景点 is different from 经典 jīngdiǎn, “classic.”
- 跟团 gēntuán - travel with a tour group. 我不喜欢跟团旅游。
- 自由行 zìyóuxíng - independent travel. 我喜欢自由行。
- 休假 xiūjià - take leave from work. 我想休假。 Unlike 退休 tuìxiū, it does not mean retiring.

4. Useful phrases

- 请再说一遍。 Qǐng zài shuō yí biàn. Please say it again.
- ……用中文怎么说？ ……yòng Zhōngwén zěnme shuō? How do you say … in Chinese?
- 我想说的是…… Wǒ xiǎng shuō de shì…… What I mean is …
- 对……不好。 Duì……bù hǎo. Be bad for … Example: 晒太久对皮肤不好。 Sunbathing too long is bad for your skin.

5. Sentence structures and drills: your main practice

- Say each model aloud, then cover it and rebuild it from the English. Change only the indicated part first.

- Drill 1: 我会……，但是不会……。 Learned skills.
- 我会游泳，但是不会冲浪。 I can swim, but I cannot surf.
- 我会滑冰，但是不会滑雪。 I can ice skate, but I cannot ski.
- Your turn: I can speak Spanish, but I cannot speak French.

- Drill 2: 我 + time + 跟……一起 + 去……旅游。 Travel plans and word order.
- 我下个月跟太太一起去欧洲旅游。 Next month I am going to Europe with my wife.
- 我九月跟家人一起去中国旅游。 In September I am going to China with my family.
- Your turn: Next year I am going to Japan with friends.

- Drill 3: 我去过…… / 我还没去过……。 Experience.
- 我去过法国一次。 I have been to France once.
- 我还没去过中国。 I have not been to China yet.
- Your turn: I have been to Spain once. I have not traveled with a tour group.
- Answer: 我去过西班牙一次。我还没跟团旅游过。

- Drill 4: ……的时候，……。 Put the situation first.
- 滑冰的时候，我的腿很疼。 When I ice skate, my legs hurt.
- 坐车的时候，我喜欢坐在前排。 When riding in a car, I like sitting in the front.
- Your turn: When traveling, I like independent travel.

- Drill 5: 人 + 住在 + 地方。 Where someone lives.
- 我爸爸住在俄罗斯。 My father lives in Russia.
- 我岳母住在秘鲁。 My mother-in-law lives in Peru.
- 我岳父住得不远，开车一个小时就到。 My father-in-law lives nearby; it takes an hour to drive there.
- Your turn: My family lives in California.

- Drill 6: 我早就想……了，但是……。 A longstanding wish.
- 我早就想休假了，但是没有时间。 I have wanted to take time off for a long time, but I have no time.
- 我早就想去中国了，但是一直没去。 I have wanted to go to China for a long time, but still have not gone.
- Your turn: I have wanted to learn surfing for a long time, but I have no time.
- 早就想 does not itself mean you cannot do something. The second clause explains what has prevented it.

6. Corrections to classroom explanations

- 冲浪 and 钓鱼 are already verbs. Say 我冲过一次浪 and 我钓过一次鱼; do not copy 我钓鱼过一次.
- For the liquid used to clean a windshield, 挡风玻璃清洗液 is clear; 汽油 means gasoline. The transcript’s 挡风叶 appears unreliable.
- 入侵物种 means an invasive species, not simply an aggressive animal.
- Words with the same pronunciation but different meanings are 同音词. 同义词 means synonyms.
- 溜冰 can also mean ice skating. 轮滑 clearly identifies skating on wheels.
- 树 shù and 书 shū differ in tone. Practice: 一本书，一棵树。我看书。我看树。
- For the family reading: 身体还非常好, rather than 身体很非常好; 岳母 yuèmǔ, rather than 爷母.
- “Lots of sunshine” is 阳光充足 or 经常有太阳. Avoid treating 太阳 as countable in 很多太阳.

7. A manageable daily routine

- Spend five minutes on drills 1–3 one day and drills 4–6 the next.
- For each structure, say the models once and make two personal sentences.
- Finish with three connected sentences: 我九月要去中国旅游。我还没去过中国。我想在北京待两个星期。
