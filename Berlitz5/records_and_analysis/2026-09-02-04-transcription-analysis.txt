1. What to focus on

- Your main difficulty is building sentences while speaking. You can read fairly complex material, but spontaneous answers often lose their word order or main verb.
- This transcript alone cannot establish your Berlitz level. It does suggest that short, reusable sentence patterns will help more than another long vocabulary list.
- Prioritize asking questions, describing work experience, and explaining who pays for something. Treat pauses and repetitions as speaking practice issues, not grammar mistakes.

2. Vocabulary: learn these 6 items

- 培训 péixùn — training. 参加培训 means attend training.
- 试用期 shìyòngqī — probation period. This is different from 培训.
- 工作经验 gōngzuò jīngyàn — work experience. 有三年的工作经验 — have three years of work experience.
- 负责 fùzé — be responsible for. 我负责这个项目。— I am responsible for this project.
- 支付 zhīfù — pay. 公司支付费用。— The company pays the costs.
- 有用 yǒuyòng — useful. 这个词很有用。— This word is useful.

3. Phrases: keep these ready to use

- 请再说一遍。— Please say it again.
- 我想再读一遍。— I want to read it again.
- 这个用中文怎么说？— How do you say this in Chinese?
- 今天网络不太稳定。— The connection is unstable today.
- 我认识这个词，但是还不会用。— I recognize this word, but I cannot use it yet.

5. Clarifications from the lesson

- 申请 means an application or to apply. 申请人 means applicant; 候选人 means candidate. 十多份申请 counts applications, not people.
- 职责 is a noun meaning duties. 负责 is a verb meaning be responsible for. Any employee can 负责 something: 我的职责是测试软件。我负责软件测试。
- 公司支付或报销餐费和住宿费 describes paying or reimbursing expenses. 补贴 is an allowance or subsidy.
- The teacher’s explanation of 倍 mixed up two patterns. 工资是原来的两倍 means twice the original salary. 工资比原来增加了两倍 means the increase equals twice the original salary, making the total three times as much.
- If “we’re at time” meant the lesson time was up, say 今天的课就到这里吧 or 时间到了. 按时 means on time.

6. Sentence structures and drills

Practice these five patterns first. Read each example aloud, then change the details without looking. Aim for a complete short sentence.

- Pattern 1: 我想 + verb; 我想再 + verb
  我想参加培训。— I want to attend training.
  我想再读一遍。— I want to read it again.
  我想再学一次。— I want to study it again.
  Your turn: Say you want to ask a question, then say you want to listen again.

- Pattern 2: Subject + verb + duration
  我参加了一个月的培训。— I attended one month of training.
  他在我们公司工作了一年半。— He worked at our company for a year and a half.
  我在美国住了很多年了。— I have lived in the US for many years.
  Your turn: Change the duration to 两个星期、三年、十年.

- Pattern 3: Question word + verb; no 吗 needed
  培训要多长时间？— How long does the training take?
  你每年有多少天年假？— How many days of annual leave do you have?
  你每年回中国几次？— How many times a year do you return to China?
  Your turn: Ask how long someone has lived in the UK: 你在英国住了多久了？

- Pattern 4: 我负责 + task; 公司支付 + expense
  我负责软件测试。— I am responsible for software testing.
  公司支付培训费用。— The company pays the training costs.
  我不用付钱，公司支付费用。— I do not need to pay; the company pays.
  Your turn: Replace 软件测试 with 这个项目, and 培训费用 with 餐费 or 住宿费.

- Pattern 5: 虽然……但是……; 因为……所以……
  虽然他的学历不高，但是工作经验很多。— Although his qualifications are not high, he has a lot of work experience.
  因为他有十年的工作经验，所以我选他。— Because he has ten years of work experience, I choose him.
  虽然工资很高，但是我不太喜欢这份工作。— Although the salary is high, I do not really like this job.
  Your turn: Explain why you would choose the third candidate in two short sentences.

7. A five-minute daily routine

- Spend one minute reviewing the six vocabulary items.
- Spend three minutes practicing the sentence drills aloud. Change one detail each time.
- Spend one minute answering: 你负责什么工作？你参加过什么培训？你为什么喜欢现在的工作？
- Start with one complete sentence per answer. Add a reason only after the first sentence comes easily.
