3. Vocabulary: learn these eight items first

- 要求 — yāoqiú — requirement. 这个职位有什么要求？ What requirements does this position have?
- 职位 — zhíwèi — job position. 工作职责 means job responsibilities, not a position.
- 工作经验 — gōngzuò jīngyàn — work experience.
- 经历 — jīnglì — an experience or something someone went through. Childhood experiences are 小时候的经历; practical knowledge gained from work is 工作经验.
- 出差 — chūchāi — travel for work.
- 年假 — niánjià — annual leave. Answer 多少天年假 with days, not money.
- 福利 — fúlì — employee benefits in this lesson. Benefits can include insurance, leave and discounts; they are not limited to completely free services.
- 医疗保险 — yīliáo bǎoxiǎn — health insurance.

4. Phrases to memorize as complete units

- 你可以再说一遍吗？ — Could you say that again?
- 我听不太清楚。 — I cannot hear clearly.
- 我明白一点，但不完全明白。 — I understand a little, but not completely.
- 我以前的经理 — my previous manager.
- 去中国出差 — travel to China for work.
- 每年有十五天年假 — have fifteen days of annual leave each year.

5. Sentence structures and drills

- Spend about ten minutes a day on these. Read each Chinese sentence aloud, then cover it and produce it from the English prompt. Finally, change one detail using your own life.

- Drill 1: Subject + time + verb + object.
  我昨天吃了拉面。 — I ate noodles yesterday.
  我今天要开会。 — I need to attend a meeting today.
  我明年想去中国。 — I want to go to China next year.
  Your turn: Say what you ate yesterday and what you need to do tomorrow.

- Drill 2: 必须会 + skill; 必须有 + qualification; 必须 + action.
  他必须会说英文。 — He must be able to speak English.
  他必须有五年的工作经验。 — He must have five years of work experience.
  他必须经常出差。 — He must travel frequently for work.
  Your turn: Give three requirements for your own job.

- Drill 3: Person + 在 + workplace + 工作了 + duration.
  我在苹果公司工作了八年。 — I have worked at Apple for eight years.
  他在这个团队工作了七八年。 — He has worked on this team for seven or eight years.
  到明年，我就在苹果公司工作满九年了。 — By next year, I will have completed nine years at Apple.
  Your turn: Describe your time at your company and your manager’s time on the team.

- Drill 4: 对……来说，……最重要。
  对我来说，健康最重要。 — Health is most important to me.
  对这个职位来说，工作经验最重要。 — Work experience is most important for this position.
  对他来说，工资多少不是最重要的。 — How much he earns is not the most important thing to him.
  工资多少 here means “how much the salary is”; the whole phrase is what the sentence discusses.
  Your turn: Make one sentence about health and one about choosing a job.

- Drill 5: 花 + amount + 买 + thing; company + 支付 + expense.
  我每个月花一千美元买医疗保险。 — I spend $1,000 a month on health insurance.
  公司支付剩下的费用。 — The company pays the remaining cost.
  租车原价每天一百美元，员工价每天五十美元。 — Car rental normally costs $100 a day; the employee rate is $50.
  Your turn: Explain one expense you pay and one benefit your company provides.

6. Small distinctions worth keeping

- 有点贵 = a little expensive. 便宜一点 = a little cheaper.
- 会说中文 = know how to speak Chinese. 可以说中文 = may speak Chinese, or can use Chinese in that situation.
- 团队 = team; 同事 = colleague. They are related but not interchangeable.
- If you meant “more and more people do not want to speak Russian,” say “越来越多的人不想说俄语。”
- If you meant “a little better,” use 更好: “如果是工商管理专业的，就更好了。” 刚好 means “just right” or “exactly.”
- The transcript’s 下班/加班, 股票/机票 and 忙/满意 may reflect slips or transcription errors. Check these pairs: 加班 = work overtime; 下班 = finish work; 机票 = plane ticket; 股票 = stock; 满意 = satisfied.

Start with drills 1–3. Add drills 4–5 once you can produce the first three without long pauses.
