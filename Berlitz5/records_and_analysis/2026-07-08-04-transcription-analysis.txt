2. Useful corrections

- “我的公司说星期五是周末。”
Better: 我们公司星期五放假。
Meaning: Our company gives us Friday off.
周末 means weekend. 放假 means have time off for a holiday.

- “我们做餐很多。”
If you mean cooking: 我们做很多菜。
If you mean barbecuing: 我们经常烧烤。
Put 很多 before the noun: 很多菜.

- “我还没有一次用农历。”
Better: 我没用过农历。
Meaning: I have never used the Chinese calendar.
For past experience, use 没 + verb + 过.

- “她有三年。”
If you mean your daughter’s age: 她三岁了。
Use 岁 for age, rather than 年.

- “盐的饺子。”
Better: 咸的饺子。
盐 (yán) is salt. 咸 (xián) means salty.

- “你在英国住在。”
Better: 你住在英国。
Pattern: person + 住在 + place.

- “在很多俄罗斯人学习中文。”
Better: 很多俄罗斯人在学中文。
Here, 在 goes before the action to describe something happening.

- “旅游中国” / “旅游法国。”
Better: 去中国旅游 / 去法国旅游。
Pattern: 去 + place + 旅游.

- “你可以听我？”
Better: 你能听到我说话吗？
Meaning: Can you hear me?
听到 describes successfully hearing something.

- “我在苹果公司八年工作。”
Better: 我在苹果公司工作了八年。
Put the duration after the verb.

- “疼很多很多。”
Better: 我太太的牙疼得很厉害。
Meaning: My wife’s tooth hurts a lot.
Use 很厉害 or 得很厉害 for intensity.

- “买两百欧元。”
If you mean the treatment cost: 看牙花了两百欧元。
花钱 means spend money; 买 means buy.

3. Vocabulary to learn first

- 放假 (fàng jià): have time off. 星期五我们放假。
- 年假 (nián jià): annual leave. 我每年有十八天年假。
- 烧烤 (shāo kǎo): barbecue. 我们喜欢烧烤。
- 烟花 (yān huā): fireworks. 看烟花 = watch fireworks; 放烟花 = set off fireworks.
- 咸 / 甜 (xián / tián): salty / sweet. 这个是咸的，那个是甜的。
- 景点 (jǐng diǎn): tourist attraction. 景点 differs from 经典 (jīng diǎn), meaning classic.
- 牙医 (yá yī): dentist. 我需要找牙医。
- 秒 / 分钟 (miǎo / fēn zhōng): second / minute. 五秒；五分钟。

4. Useful phrases

- 这个用中文怎么说？
How do you say this in Chinese?

- 我不知道这个用中文怎么说。
I don’t know how to say this in Chinese.

- 我想说的是……
What I mean is…
Example: 我想说的是冬天。

- 我可以共享屏幕吗？
Can I share my screen?

- 我们休息五分钟吧。
Let’s take a five-minute break.

5. Sentence structures and drills

Spend most of your practice time here. Read each sentence aloud, then cover the Chinese and rebuild it from the English. Repeat until you can say it without a long pause.

- Experience: 我 + verb + 过…… / 我没 + verb + 过……
我吃过饺子。I have eaten dumplings.
我没吃过汤圆。I have never eaten tangyuan.
我去过法国。I have been to France.
我还没去过中国。I haven’t been to China yet.

- Place and duration: 我在 + place + verb + 了 + duration
我在罗马住了七天。I stayed in Rome for seven days.
我在法国住了两个星期。I stayed in France for two weeks.
我在苹果公司工作了八年。I worked at Apple for eight years.
For an ongoing job: 我在苹果公司工作八年了。I have been working at Apple for eight years.

- Plans: time + 我打算 + action
明年我打算去中国旅游。I plan to travel to China next year.
九月我们打算在中国待四个星期。We plan to spend four weeks in China in September.
我打算带女儿去迪士尼乐园。I plan to take my daughter to Disneyland.

- Holiday activities: ……的时候，我们……
独立日的时候，我们烧烤。On Independence Day, we barbecue.
晚上的时候，我们看烟花。In the evening, we watch fireworks.
中秋节的时候，人们吃月饼。During the Mid-Autumn Festival, people eat mooncakes.

- Contrast: ……，但是……
这个粽子是咸的，但是那个是甜的。This zongzi is salty, but that one is sweet.
我去过法国，但是没去过意大利。I have been to France, but not Italy.
我想吃土鸡，但是土鸡很贵。I want to eat locally raised chicken, but it is expensive.

6. Teacher explanations to avoid memorizing

- 强壮 usually describes physical strength in people or animals. For your fireworks explanation, say: 那种烟花能飞得很高。Those fireworks can fly very high.
- 免费 means free of charge. If you mean extra days off that do not use annual leave, say: 公司另外给我们一周假，不用扣年假。
- 耶稣的生日 corresponds to 圣诞节, Christmas. 复活节 is Easter and commemorates the resurrection.
- Communism is 共产主义. 社会主义 means socialism.
- “只能在元宵节吃汤圆” is too absolute. A useful practice sentence is: 人们通常在元宵节吃汤圆。People usually eat tangyuan during the Lantern Festival.

7. Ten-minute practice routine

- Practice the experience sentences for three minutes.
- Practice place and duration for three minutes.
- Use the plan sentences to describe your China trip for three minutes.
- Finish with: 我还没去过中国。明年九月，我打算跟家人一起去中国旅游。我们打算在中国待四个星期。
