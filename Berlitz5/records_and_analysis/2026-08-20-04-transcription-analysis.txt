1. What to focus on

- You can discuss complex ideas, but you often get stuck building basic sentences. Your priority should be short, complete sentences about places, times, experiences, and opinions.
- You already used these correctly: 我最喜欢的城市是……, 我怕吃河豚, 我不想吃河豚, and 我们休息五分钟。
- This transcript alone is not enough to assign a Berlitz level. It does show that practicing basic sentence patterns would help more than learning many advanced political or geographical terms.
- Some passages are unclear or may contain transcription errors. The corrections below focus on clear examples.

2. Main corrections

- 00:03: 从德国我们去荷兰。
Better for recounting this trip: 我们先去了德国，然后去了荷兰。
English: We first went to Germany, then went to the Netherlands.
去过 describes experience; 去了 describes a particular completed visit. 我们去过五个国家 is grammatical, but 这次我们去了五个国家 fits this trip better.

- 00:05: 外国人旅游中国越来越大。
Better: 去中国旅游的外国人越来越多。
English: More and more foreigners are traveling to China.
Use 多 for the number of people. Say 去中国旅游.

- 00:13: 如果他们在美国住宅……
Better: 如果他们住在纽约，就可以……
English: If they live in New York, they can…
住宅 is a noun meaning a residence. 住 is the verb: 住在 + place.

- 00:19: 阿拉木图……不能看。
Better: 在这张地图上看不到阿拉木图。
English: You cannot see Almaty on this map.
看不到 means it is not visible. 不能看 usually means you cannot or are not allowed to look.

- 00:21: 夜间的时候，我听一个地震。
If you mean you experienced the shaking: 小时候，有一天晚上，我感觉到了地震。
English: When I was little, one evening I felt an earthquake.
Use 感觉到 for feeling the shaking. 听说 is for hearing news about something.

- 00:31: 我的家可以有发生水灾。
Better: 我家附近可能会发生水灾。
English: There may be flooding near my home.
Use 可能会 for a possibility. Remove 有 before 发生.

- 00:32: 我的家很近。
Better: 我家离这条河很近。
English: My home is close to this river.
The pattern is A 离 B 很近.

- 00:43: 我在起床的时候……我读我的手机。
Better: 今天早上起床以后，我在手机上看到了一条新闻。
English: After getting up this morning, I saw a news story on my phone.
在手机上 tells us where you read it. 起床以后 means after getting up.

- 00:47: 他们做很好……很好的生菜。
If you mean raw dishes: 他们做的生食很好。
English: The raw food they prepare is very good.
生菜 means lettuce. 生食 means raw food.

- 00:48–00:49: 我的朋友生病了……以前吃 Mikuni。
Better: 我的朋友在 Mikuni 吃饭以后生病了。
English: My friend became ill after eating at Mikuni.
以前 means before or previously. 以后 means after. 生病了 does not by itself establish that someone had 食物中毒.

- 01:04: 我没有观点，如果……是好还是不好。
Better: 我还没有明确的看法。我不知道它好不好。
English: I do not have a clear opinion yet. I do not know whether it is good.
Do not use 如果 for “whether” here.

- 01:15: 我要一个问题。你不知道说广东话？
Better: 我想问一个问题。你会说广东话吗？
English: I would like to ask a question. Can you speak Cantonese?
Use 会 for a learned skill. 不知道 means “do not know”; 不会 means “do not know how.”

3. Vocabulary to learn first

- 发生 — fāshēng — to happen: 发生地震, 发生水灾。
- 可能 — kěnéng — possibly; may: 可能会下雨。
- 经历 — jīnglì — to experience: 我没有经历过水灾。
- 感觉到 — gǎnjué dào — to feel or sense: 我感觉到了地震。
- 听说 — tīngshuō — to hear about: 我听说过这件事。
- 看法 — kànfǎ — opinion: 你有什么看法？
- 过期 — guòqī — to expire; expired: 牛奶过期了。
- 生食 — shēngshí — raw food. Compare 生菜 — shēngcài — lettuce.

4. Useful phrases

- 我想问一个问题。— Wǒ xiǎng wèn yí ge wèntí. — I would like to ask a question.
- 我不知道怎么用中文说。— Wǒ bù zhīdào zěnme yòng Zhōngwén shuō. — I do not know how to say it in Chinese.
- 我的意思是…… — Wǒ de yìsi shì… — What I mean is…
- 我还没有明确的看法。— Wǒ hái méiyǒu míngquè de kànfǎ. — I do not have a clear opinion yet.

5. Sentence structures and drills

Spend about ten minutes a day on these. Read each example aloud, then cover the Chinese and say it from the English. Change one detail each time.

- Experience versus a particular event: 去过 / 去了
我去过英国。— I have been to the UK.
这次我去了英国。— I went to the UK on this trip.
我没去过东京。— I have never been to Tokyo.
Practice with 德国、荷兰、奥地利.

- Location and distance: 住在…… / A 离 B 很近
我住在加州。— I live in California.
我家离这条河很近。— My home is close to this river.
这家饭馆离我家很远。— This restaurant is far from my home.
Practice with 学校、机场、博物馆.

- Possibility: 地方 + 可能会发生 + 事情
这里可能会发生水灾。— Flooding may happen here.
这里可能会发生地震。— An earthquake may happen here.
我家附近可能会发生水灾。— Flooding may happen near my home.
Remember the whole phrase 可能会发生.

- Sequence: 做某事以后，……
起床以后，我看了新闻。— After getting up, I read the news.
吃饭以后，他生病了。— After eating, he became ill.
去了德国以后，我们又去了荷兰。— After visiting Germany, we also visited the Netherlands.
Practice with 喝咖啡、下课、回家.

- Increasing numbers: ……的人越来越多
去中国旅游的人越来越多。— More and more people are traveling to China.
这家饭馆的客人越来越多。— This restaurant has more and more customers.
学中文的人越来越多。— More and more people are learning Chinese.

- Ability and uncertainty: 会不会…… / 不知道……好不好
你会不会说广东话？— Can you speak Cantonese?
我会说一点中文。— I can speak a little Chinese.
我不知道这家饭馆好不好。— I do not know whether this restaurant is good.
我不知道这个词怎么说。— I do not know how to say this word.

6. One short speaking drill

- 这次我们去了五个国家。我们先去了德国，然后去了荷兰。我很喜欢英国。我最喜欢的城市是伦敦。明年我想去中国。我打算十月去。
- Say this once while reading, then again without looking. Finally, change the countries, city, and month to make a new version. Aim for complete sentences before adding more detail.

7. A correction to the teacher’s explanation

- Mushroom color and appearance do not reliably show whether a mushroom is poisonous. Do not learn “colorful means poisonous” or “plain means safe” as a rule.
