1. What to focus on

- You can communicate ideas and ask useful questions. 我今天很忙, 我没有时间看报纸, and 我不想说 are good sentences.
- Your biggest challenge in this lesson is building complete sentences while speaking. Focus on a few reusable patterns before adding more vocabulary.
- I cannot judge your overall Berlitz level from one transcript. For this lesson, shorter sentences and repeated speaking drills would be more useful than memorizing every new word.

3. Vocabulary: learn these six items first

- 听到 tīngdào — hear something. 我没听到。 I didn’t hear it.
- 告诉 gàosu — tell someone. 他告诉我了。 He told me. The lesson material’s guosu was a typo.
- 事情 shìqing — matter, thing to do. 我有很多事情要做。 I have many things to do.
- 相信 xiāngxìn — believe, trust. 我不太相信这个消息。 I don’t really believe this news.
- 发生 fāshēng — happen. 发生了什么？ What happened?
- 进 jìn / 近 jìn — enter / near. Same pronunciation, different characters and meanings. 请进。 Please come in. 很近。 Very close.

4. Phrases: keep these ready to use

- 你听说了吗？ Nǐ tīngshuō le ma? — Have you heard?
- 真的吗？ Zhēn de ma? — Really?
- 你怎么知道的？ Nǐ zěnme zhīdào de? — How did you find out?
- 不好意思，占用了你的时间。 Bù hǎoyìsi, zhànyòng le nǐ de shíjiān. — Sorry for taking up your time.

我不想说 is already a natural way to decline a question. 无可奉告 means “no comment” and sounds more formal.

5. Sentence structures and drills: your main practice

Spend five to ten minutes a day on these four groups. Read each sentence aloud, then cover the Chinese and say it from the English. Repeat each group until you can say it without long pauses.

- Pattern 1: 听得到 / 没听到 — being able to hear / not having heard
  - 你听得到吗？ — Can you hear?
  - 我听得到。 — I can hear.
  - 我没听到。 — I didn’t hear.
  - 我没听到，请再说一遍。 — I didn’t hear; please say it again.

- Pattern 2: 在 + place + action — where you do something
  - 我在手机上看新闻。 — I read news on my phone.
  - 我在电脑上看新闻。 — I read news on my computer.
  - 我在家学中文。 — I study Chinese at home.
  - 我在公司工作。 — I work at the office.

- Pattern 3: 是……的 — identifying who provided information
  - 谁告诉你的？ — Who told you?
  - 是同事告诉我的。 — A colleague told me.
  - 你听谁说的？ — Who did you hear it from?
  - 我是听老师说的。 — I heard it from my teacher.

In 是同事告诉我的, 同事 is the person telling, and 我 is the person receiving the information. 是……的 highlights the source. Learn the whole sentence first; you do not need to master every use of 的 now.

- Pattern 4: 有 / 没有时间 + action; 要 + action — time and tasks
  - 我今天有时间学中文。 — I have time to study Chinese today.
  - 我今天没有时间看新闻。 — I don’t have time to read news today.
  - 我今天要做很多事情。 — I have many things to do today.
  - 我今天很忙，因为我要做很多事情。 — I’m busy today because I have many things to do.

6. Clarifications from the teacher’s explanations

- 高兴 and 生气 are not exact opposites. 不高兴 means unhappy; 生气 specifically means angry.
- 谣言 means a rumor or an unsubstantiated claim circulated as fact. It is not simply another word for all fake news.
- For a park entrance, learn 入口 and 出口. Use 进口 and 出口 for importing and exporting goods.
- 潮湿 means damp or humid; it does not necessarily mean rainy. Its opposite is 干燥, not 热.
