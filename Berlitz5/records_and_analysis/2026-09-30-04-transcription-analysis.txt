1. What to focus on

- You can communicate preferences and personal experiences, but longer sentences often lose their structure. Your priority should be short, complete sentences that you can produce without translating every word.
- You often recognize a word after a hint but cannot recall it independently. Practise saying words inside sentences, especially 质量、耐用、品牌、型号.
- This transcript alone cannot establish your Berlitz level. It does show that basic sentence patterns need more practice before longer explanations.
- Some transcript wording may be inaccurate. The corrections below focus on clear examples.

2. Important corrections

- “我还没有工作” means “I don’t have a job yet.” For “I haven’t started work yet,” say 我今天还没开始工作。
- “我每天我要上课……晚上六点、七点。” → 我每天上午上课，然后工作到晚上六七点。 Work until a time: 工作到……
- “但是我没有很多人说。” If you mean you told few people: 但是我没告诉很多人。 If few people wished you happy birthday: 但是祝我生日快乐的人不多。
- “我觉得这个是好吃的。” → 我觉得这个很好吃。 Use the adjective directly here.
- “是什么肠粉？” → 肠粉是什么？ For “What is X?” use X是什么？
- “因为中国旅游的时候……” → 因为我去中国旅游的时候，想看懂菜单。 看懂 means to read and understand.
- “我读再一次。” → 我再读一次。 再 comes before the verb.
- “Toyota很难用。” You appear to mean 丰田车很耐用。 耐用 nàiyòng means durable; 难用 nányòng means difficult to use. The teacher’s response in this section also appears to confuse them.
- “他们不想帮忙你，他们想做钱。” → 他们不想帮你，只想赚钱。 Use 帮你 and 赚钱.
- “我买每个东西都贵的。” → 我买东西都买贵的。 This means “Whenever I buy things, I choose expensive ones.”
- “这个是二手店买的。” → 这是我在二手店买的。 Include 在 for the place of purchase.

3. Vocabulary: learn these eight first

- 质量 zhìliàng - quality. 质量很好。
- 耐用 nàiyòng - durable. 这辆车很耐用。
- 品牌 pǐnpái - brand. 丰田是品牌。
- 型号 xínghào - model. Camry是型号。
- 保修 bǎoxiū - warranty coverage. 这台空调保修三年。
- 付款 fùkuǎn - pay. 我用信用卡付款。
- 赚钱 zhuànqián - earn money. 他们只想赚钱。
- 二手 èrshǒu - secondhand. 我买了一本二手书。

4. Phrases to remember as complete chunks

- 我打算…… wǒ dǎsuàn…… - I plan to……
- 还不错 hái búcuò - pretty good.
- 签合同 qiān hétong - sign a contract. For documents generally, use 签文件.
- 请再说一遍。 Qǐng zài shuō yí biàn. - Please say it again.
- 这个词是什么意思？ Zhège cí shì shénme yìsi? - What does this word mean?

5. Sentence structures and drills

- Practise the following five groups. Read each sentence aloud, then cover the Chinese and say it from the English. Finally, change one detail.

- Structure 1: Subject + time + plan + action.
- 我今天打算买一个蛋糕。 - I plan to buy a cake today.
- 我下个月打算买一辆新车。 - I plan to buy a new car next month.
- 我明年打算去中国旅游。 - I plan to travel to China next year.
- Change 今天、下个月、明年. Remember 一辆车.

- Structure 2: 再 + verb + 一次.
- 我再读一次。 - I’ll read it again.
- 我再看一次。 - I’ll look again.
- 我再说一次。 - I’ll say it again.
- Say these quickly enough that 再 naturally comes before the verb.

- Structure 3: A + 比 + B + adjective.
- 这辆车比那辆车贵。 - This car is more expensive than that car.
- 这台空调比那台空调省电。 - This air conditioner uses less electricity than that one.
- 这个品牌的质量比那个品牌的质量好。 - This brand’s quality is better than that brand’s quality.
- Change the objects first; keep the sentence pattern.

- Structure 4: 虽然……但是……
- 虽然这辆车很贵，但是很耐用。 - Although this car is expensive, it is durable.
- 虽然这些东西很便宜，但是质量很好。 - Although these things are cheap, their quality is good.
- 虽然我认识这个字，但是不知道怎么读。 - Although I recognize this character, I don’t know how to pronounce it.
- Use this pattern to explain your opinion in two short parts.

- Structure 5: 是 + person + 在 + place + verb + 的.
- 这本书是我在二手店买的。 - I bought this book at a secondhand shop.
- 这些点心是我在Costco买的。 - I bought this dim sum at Costco.
- 这辆车是我在网上买的。 - I bought this car online.
- Use this pattern when explaining where you bought something.

6. Your daily practice

- Spend five minutes on one drill group each day.
- Say the three examples, then make two sentences about your own life.
- Finish by telling this short version of your dealership story:
- 我去车行买车。 - I went to a dealership to buy a car.
- 签合同的时候，我太太发现价钱不一样。 - While we were signing the contract, my wife noticed the price was different.
- 他们多收了一千五百美元。 - They charged an extra 1,500 dollars.
- 我说：“我没说要买这些东西。” - I said, “I didn’t say I wanted to buy these things.”
