1. What to focus on

You can express opinions and ask follow-up questions, but sentence order and recalling words slow you down. Your most useful practice is short sentences about work and everyday life. This transcript alone is not enough to assign you a precise level.

Focus on five sentence patterns below. Practise saying complete sentences without adding extra details first.

2. Important corrections

- You said: 我在苹果公司五年工作。
Correct: 我在苹果公司工作了五年了。
Meaning: I have been working at Apple for five years.
Put the duration after the verb. The final 了 makes it clear that you still work there.

- You said several times: 我读再一次。
Correct: 我再读一次。
Meaning: I’ll read it again.
再 goes before the verb.

- You said: 如果我不明白，我说你。
Correct: 如果我不明白，我就问你。
Meaning: If I don’t understand, I’ll ask you.
If you mean “I’ll tell you,” use 我就告诉你.

- You said: 他不能打电话你。
Correct: 他不能给你打电话吗？
Meaning: Can’t he call you?
Use 给 + person + 打电话.

- You said: 你不看你的爸爸一年。
Correct: 你一年没见到你爸爸吗？
Meaning: You didn’t see your father for a year?
Use 见到 for seeing or meeting a person in this context.

- You said: 我还没有认识一个人做核武器工程师。
More natural: 我还没认识过做核武器研究的人。
Meaning: I haven’t met anyone who works in nuclear weapons research.
没……过 expresses an experience you haven’t had.

- You said: 我们总结了我的开会。
Correct: 我们总结了会议的内容。
Meaning: We summarized the meeting’s content.
开会 is an action; 会议 is the noun.

- You said: 我们休息五分。
Correct: 我们休息五分钟吧。
Meaning: Let’s take a five-minute break.

3. Clarifications from the lesson

- 级别 means a defined level or rank. 评级 means assigning or assessing a rating. 水平 means ability or proficiency. For your workplace, 我升到ICT4了 is a simple way to say “I was promoted to ICT4.” 评职称 applies specifically to professional title assessment and may not describe your company’s process.

- 对……有意见 and 给……提意见 are related but different. 我对这个决定有意见 means “I have objections to this decision.” 我给经理提了意见 means “I gave the manager feedback.” Having objections does not necessarily mean you expressed them.

- 交流 means exchanging information or ideas. It does not simply mean “inform.” 商量 means discussing what to do, often with a decision in mind.

- 处理问题 is normal Chinese. 处理 means handling something; 解决 means resolving it. 处理 does not inherently have a negative meaning.

- Scientist is 科学家, not 科学. For a nuclear test, 核试验 is more natural than 核武器考试.

- For “He still speaks Russian,” say 他还会说俄语. 还 expresses “still” here; 会 expresses a learned ability.

Some passages, including 主宰了 and 爱科了, are unclear in the transcript. I would not treat them as definite errors without knowing what you intended.

4. Vocabulary to learn first

- 级别, jíbié: level or rank. 公司有五个级别。
- 方案, fāng’àn: proposed plan or option. 第二个方案比较好。
- 商量, shāngliang: discuss what to do. 我想跟你商量一下。
- 交流, jiāoliú: exchange ideas or information. 我们交流一下经验。
- 总结, zǒngjié: summarize. 我来总结一下。
- 处理, chǔlǐ: handle. 我们怎么处理这个问题？

Learn these six first. Keep the other unfamiliar words for later.

5. Useful phrases

- 我觉得……有道理。I think … makes sense.
- 我对……有意见。I have objections to …
- 我想给……提个意见。I’d like to give … some feedback.
- 就这么决定吧。Let’s settle on that.

6. Sentence structures and drills

- Duration: subject + 在 + place + verb + 了 + duration + 了
我在苹果公司工作了五年了。
我在这里住了三年了。
我学中文学了一年了。
Your turn: Say how long you have worked, lived somewhere, and studied Chinese.

- Repeating an action: subject + 再 + verb + 一次
我再读一次。
请你再说一次。
我们再讨论一次。
Your turn: Say “I’ll look again” and “I’ll ask again.”

- Giving something to someone: 给 + person + action
我给经理打电话。
我给老师发消息。
我给老板提意见。
Your turn: Change the person in each sentence.

- Asking how to handle something: 我们 + verb + 一下 + question
我们商量一下怎么处理这个问题。
我们讨论一下选哪个方案。
我们商量一下什么时候开会。
Your turn: Ask to discuss where to eat and when to leave.

- Contrast with “still”: 虽然……，但是……还……
虽然大家都反对，但是我还是觉得这个方案很好。
虽然他退休了，但是他还在学习。
虽然我学过这个词，但是我还是不太会用。
Your turn: Say “Although I’m busy, I still want to study Chinese.”

7. Daily speaking practice

- Spend five minutes on these drills. Read each sentence aloud, change one detail, then say your version without looking.
- Finish by saying these four sentences together:

我以前是ICT3，现在是ICT4。
