Mistakes to Address

1. Adding 很 in a 比 comparison

You said: 五月的飞机票比八月的飞机票很贵
Your teacher corrected this clearly: you cannot add 很 in a 比 comparison. 比 already establishes the comparison. The result adjective stands alone.
Correct: 五月的飞机票比八月的飞机票贵
This is a very consistent error and one to stop immediately. There is no 很 after 比 + noun.

2. 比较更 together

You said: 因为机票比较更便宜
These two words do not combine. You use one or the other.
Use 比较便宜 to mean "relatively cheaper" or "comparatively cheaper."
Use 更便宜 to mean "even cheaper" (implying a direct comparison is already in context).
Do not say 比较更.

3. 我不可以 vs 我不能

Several times you used 我不可以 where 我不能 is more natural.
不可以 tends to mean "it is not permitted" (a rule is being broken).
不能 means "I cannot" (ability or circumstance).
When talking about inability to distinguish characters or find restaurants, 不能 is correct.
Correct: 我不能区分这些汉字

4. 呆在家里 vs 待在家里

Your teacher used 待在家里 (to stay home). In standard written Mandarin, 待 is the correct character for this meaning. 呆 (the stiff or dazed sense) is sometimes used in informal speech, but for your level, use 待在家里.

5. 一个机票 vs 一张机票

You said 一个机票 twice before being corrected.
The classifier for flat objects like tickets, paper, photos, and tables is 张, not 个.
Correct: 一张机票
This builds on the March 30 classifier note. The key ones to drill are 张 (flat objects) and 辆 (vehicles).

6. Saying 汉字们

You said 汉字们 to mean "these characters."
们 is a plural suffix used only for people, not for objects or characters.
Correct ways: 这些汉字, 这几个汉字, 那几个汉字

Sentence Structures and Drills

This is the core of the lesson. These patterns are the highest priority to drill out loud.


1. 比 comparison - no 很

Pattern: A + 比 + B + adjective (no 很, no 非常)

The mistake you made consistently is adding 很 inside this structure. Remove it.

- 韩国比澳大利亚便宜。
- 五月的飞机票比八月的飞机票贵。
- 老陈比我有经验。
- 北京的春天比夏天好。

Drill: Pick four pairs from your life (cities, prices, seasons, people) and make one 比 sentence for each. Check that you have not put 很 between 比 and the adjective.


2. 比较 + adjective (without a stated second item)

Pattern: subject + 比较 + adjective
Meaning: relatively, comparatively (a second item is implied, not stated)

- 八月的机票比较便宜。
- 老陈比较有经验。
- 和那个东西比，这个东西比较贵。
- 我比较喜欢喝咖啡。

Difference from 比: with 比 you state both items explicitly (A比B贵). With 比较 you imply a comparison without naming both sides every time.

Drill: Take the four 比 sentences you made above and convert each one into a 比较 sentence.


3. 决定 as verb and as noun

As a verb: subject + 决定 + verb phrase
As a noun: 我的决定是 + verb phrase

- 我决定去中国旅游。
- 我决定先待在家里。
- 我的决定是八月再去中国旅游，因为机票比较便宜。
- 他们决定让老陈负责这个项目。

Drill: State one real decision you have made recently using the verb form, then restate it using the noun form. For example: 我决定在家工作。我的决定是在家工作，因为...


4. 必须 and 不需要 contrast

Pattern: subject + 必须 + verb phrase
Opposite: subject + 不需要 + verb phrase

- 人必须要喝水。
- 开会以前，秘书必须把要用的文件准备好。
- 我周末不需要加班。
- 因为特朗普，我的旅游预算必须减少。

Drill: Think of three things in your life that are obligatory and three that are not required. Make one 必须 sentence and one 不需要 sentence for each pair.


5. 把 with result complements - specific patterns from this lesson

These three 把 patterns appeared in this class. They build on what was covered in March 30 and April 3.

把...减百分之... - reduce something by a percentage
把...赶出来 - to rush something out (time pressure is implied)
把...准备好 - to get something ready, to prepare something properly

Drill sentences:

- 客户要把预算减百分之十。
- 因为特朗普，我必须把旅游预算减少。
- 他周末要加班，把计划书赶出来。
- 开会以前，秘书必须把要用的文件准备好。
- 请把晚餐准备好。

Generate two sentences of your own using each of the three patterns.
