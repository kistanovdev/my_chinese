Your biggest opportunity is making familiar words into ready-to-say sentence patterns. You often understood a correction and repeated it successfully; the same patterns then caused difficulty again in spontaneous speech. Prioritize these ten.

1. **Put frequency words before the verb.**  
   “可以吃土豆每天” → **她妈妈可以每天吃土豆。**  
   “不能吃每天” → **我不能每天吃土豆。**  
   If you mean you *don’t want to*, say **我喜欢吃土豆，但是不想每天吃。**  
   **她不是每天都吃土豆。** = She doesn’t eat potatoes every day.

2. **Use 有点 + adjective; adjective + 一点 for comparison.**  
   “一点贵” → **有点贵。** = A little expensive.  
   “一点安静” → **比较安静。** = Fairly quiet.  
   **安静一点** means “a little quieter.” **有点** often introduces an undesirable quality, so **有点贵／有点吵** are especially useful.  
   “一点车” → **车不多。**

3. **Use 过 for experience, and put 次 after the verb.**  
   “我吃了黑土豆” → **我吃过黑土豆。** when discussing experience. Your original is grammatical for a particular completed meal.  
   “还没有看……黑土豆” → **我没见过黑土豆。**  
   “我很多很多搬家” → **我搬过很多次家。**  
   **我请过两次搬家公司。** = I’ve hired movers twice.

4. **Keep location and duration patterns separate.**  
   “我在西萨克拉门托住在” → **我住在西萨克拉门托。**  
   “可以住在一年” → **可以住一年。**  
   **我从圣何塞搬到了圣克拉拉。**  
   Patterns: **住在 + place；住 + duration；从 A 搬到 B。**

5. **一定 expresses certainty; 一定要 expresses a strong requirement or intention.**  
   “你一定住在……” → **你一定要住在这里。** if you mean “You must live here.”  
   Compare:

   - **他一定住在这里。** — He surely lives here.
   - **他一定要住在这里。** — He must live here / insists on living here.
   - **你不用出去。** — You don’t need to go out.
   - **你不能出去。** — You cannot / may not go out.

   For your cat example, **猫不用每天出去。** expresses lack of necessity. **不要出去** usually tells someone not to go out. These are language examples, not verification of the rental rules discussed.

6. **Use 比 + adjective without 很.**  
   “中国语法比俄文语法很容易” → **中文语法比俄语语法容易。**  
   You can also say **中文语法比俄语语法容易多了。** = Much easier.  
   **住在市区比较方便。** is natural when the comparison is understood.

7. **Connect nouns with 和; introduce another statement with 也.**  
   “我要鸡肉……米饭” → **我想吃鸡肉和米饭。**  
   “我的太太，也我的阿姨，一点” → **我太太帮我打包，我阿姨也帮了一点忙。**  
   **我太太和我阿姨帮我打包。** is correct if you don’t need to specify how much each helped.

8. **Choose the practical verb instead of relying on 做／有／买.**  
   “买人打包” → **请人打包。**  
   “洗你的家” → **打扫房子。**  
   “不想有狗” → **不想养狗。** Your original is understandable; **养狗** specifically means keeping and caring for a dog.  
   “做新家” → **建新房子。**  
   For housing: **房客租房子；房东把房子出租给房客。**  
   **搬家公司** helps you move; **房产中介** helps you find or rent housing.

9. **Give 把 an object and a clear action or result.**  
   “搬家以前，我们把，打包” → **搬家以前，我们把东西打包好。**  
   Useful lesson patterns:

   - **把不要的东西扔掉。** — Throw unwanted things away.
   - **把东西放进箱子里。** — Put things into boxes.
   - **把东西整理好。** — Finish arranging things.

   **扔掉** means throw away; **送给别人** means give to someone.  
   “我们……打包……四个星期” → **我们花了四个星期打包。**  
   Pattern: **花了 + time + action。**

10. **Memorize these reusable conversation chunks.**  
    “很多房子看一样” → **很多房子看起来一样。**  
    “对我的来说” → **对我来说。**  
    “你可以说再一次” → **你可以再说一次吗？**  
    “我是读没有拼音的” → **我想试着不看拼音读。**  
    Your **我忘了怎么说** was already natural—keep using it.

**Useful vocabulary distinctions**

- **麻烦** — troublesome, a hassle; **难／困难** — difficult. Moving can be both.
- **重** (*zhòng*) — heavy; **重要** (*zhòngyào*) — important. You noticed and corrected this distinction yourself.
- **种** — kinds: **三四种土豆**; **本** — books: **一百本书**; **套** — homes: **一套房子**.
- **几条狗** can mean “several dogs” in a statement, or “how many dogs?” in a question.
- **睡不好觉** — cannot sleep well; **睡不着** — cannot fall asleep.
- **垃圾** — *lājī* in mainland Mandarin; **辣** — *là*, spicy.

**Transcription uncertainty**

Without audio, pronunciation cannot be assessed reliably. **“龘”** in the 搬家 exchange is almost certainly a transcription error. **“北极昌”** is probably a garbled facility name, but the exact word is unclear. **“没有鲜”** and **“搬家之力”** are too unclear to treat as definite learner mistakes.

Written **他／她／它** all represent the same spoken *tā*, so the transcript’s pronoun characters are not evidence of your errors. **“我跟我的太太长大，从12岁”** may contain a transcription error or a spoken slip; the surrounding discussion suggests your aunt, but does not establish the exact wording. In contrast, repeated word-order problems such as **吃每天、住在一年、对我的来说** are much more likely to reflect actual speaking difficulties.

**Say-aloud drills**

Say each sentence twice, then make the suggested substitution.

1. **我喜欢吃土豆，但是不想每天吃。**  
   Swap 土豆 → 米饭.

2. **搬家公司有点贵，所以我自己打包。**  
   Swap 打包 → 搬家.

3. **我搬过很多次家，也请过两次搬家公司。**

4. **我住在这里。我想在这里住三年。**  
   Swap 三年 → 一年.

5. **你一定要出去吗？我今天不用出去。**

6. **市区比郊区方便，但是郊区比较安静。**

7. **我太太帮我打包，我阿姨也帮了一点忙。**

8. **搬家以前，我把东西打包好，再把不要的东西扔掉。**

9. **我们花了四个星期打包，花了三个月整理东西。**

10. **对我来说，这两个字看起来很像。你可以再说一次吗？**
