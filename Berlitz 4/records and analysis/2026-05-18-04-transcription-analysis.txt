Your strongest moments came after repetition: **我不常去银行、我每个月去一次、我没去过中国** were all correct. The main speaking priority is retrieving familiar sentence patterns quickly, especially word order and choosing a precise verb instead of **做、发、送**.

Below, “→” gives natural Mandarin for the meaning supported by the conversation. Pauses and abandoned starts are not counted as mistakes.

1. **Frequency: 每＋time period＋verb＋一次**  
   “我每个月一次去银行” → **我每个月去一次银行。**  
   Ask: **你多久去一次银行？**  
   You used this correctly earlier; focus on making it automatic. **每次** means “each time,” while **每个月** means “every month.”

2. **Money verbs: 存钱、取钱、转账、付账单**  
   “发现金到银行” → **把现金存到银行账户里。**  
   “把钱转账银行” → **把钱转到银行账户里。**  
   **存钱** = deposit/save; **取钱** = withdraw; **转账** = transfer between accounts; **付账单** = pay bills. Your ATM explanation appears to concern depositing cash, so the teacher’s suggestion **转账** may not capture your meaning. **发工资** is correct for paying wages; **发钱** is not the general verb for every money transaction.

3. **不 for states/habits; 没（有）for things that haven’t happened**  
   “我没有饿” → **我不饿。**  
   **我不吃晚饭。** = I don’t eat dinner / won’t eat dinner.  
   **我还没吃晚饭。** = I haven’t eaten dinner yet.  
   For your lunch explanation: **如果午饭吃得饱，我就不饿。**  
   Here **吃得饱** means eating enough to feel full.

4. **以前、以后、……的时候**  
   You repeatedly used **以后** while discussing the past.  
   For the ID story: **以前换钱不用身份证，现在要。**  
   **以前** = previously; **以后** = later/in the future; **十年前** = ten years ago.  
   “我在俄罗斯长的” → **我在俄罗斯长大。**  
   A reusable opening: **我小的时候……**

5. **记得 versus 认为／觉得**  
   You explicitly asked whether **认为** means “remember.”  
   **我记得小时候在医院要等很久。** = I remember waiting a long time at the hospital as a child.  
   **我觉得……／我认为……** = I think / believe…  
   Use **记得** to introduce memories; **觉得** to introduce opinions.

6. **排队 is already a verb; duration follows the verb**  
   “不喜欢做排队” → **不喜欢排队。**  
   “我讨厌站很多” → **我讨厌站很久。**  
   **排队要等多久？——要等一个小时。**  
   Use **分钟** for minutes: **等五分钟、等十分钟**. **很多** describes quantity; **很久** describes duration.

7. **Location and time usually come before the main verb**  
   “我附近有我的Chase银行” → **我家附近有一家Chase银行。**  
   “它们开每天” → **它们每天都开门。**  
   This corrects the word order of your statement; it does not establish actual bank hours.  
   **一家银行／哪家银行** uses **家** for the business. **你去什么银行？** is also acceptable.

8. **Natural adjective combinations**  
   “很多风／很重风” → **风很大。**  
   “还没有很热” → **还不太热。** — for “It isn’t very hot yet.”  
   **今天很暖和。昨天风很大。**  
   For weight: **我的体重比较高。** is more natural than “高身体重.”  
   If describing a trend: **我最近吃得越来越少。** If describing an intention: **我想少吃一点。**

9. **Comparisons: A＋比＋B＋adjective**  
   “比美国的公司不好” → **比美国公司的股票差。** — if stock performance is what you mean.  
   **A比B好／差** = A is better/worse than B. Avoid **比……不好** for “worse than.”  
   Your **我觉得Chase银行的服务比较好** was correct: **比较好** means relatively good, without naming a specific comparison.

10. **把＋object＋换成／用＋object＋当**  
   For the currency dialogue: **我想把欧元换成人民币。**  
   **请问今天的汇率是多少？**  
   For your ID explanation: **我们用驾照当身份证明。**  
   This expresses using a driver’s license as proof of identity, without equating it with a passport. **身份证明** is broader than **身份证**.

**Useful lesson vocabulary to keep active:** **银行账户** (bank account), **开户／开账户** (open an account), **现金** (cash), **网上银行** (online banking), **银行卡** (bank card), **自动提款机** (ATM), **填表** (fill out a form), **证件** (identity documents). In **银行**, 行 is pronounced **háng**; in **行不行**, it is **xíng**.
