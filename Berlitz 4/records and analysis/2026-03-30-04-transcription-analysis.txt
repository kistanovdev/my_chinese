Mistakes to Address

1. Wrong connector with 告诉

Correct: 我告诉你
告诉 already takes a direct object (the person you are telling). Do not add 跟 between 告诉 and the person. The structure is 我告诉你 + what you are telling them, for example 我告诉你一件事。

3. Using 和 instead of 或者 for "or"

You said: 六百和七百汉字
Correct: 六百或者七百个汉字
和 means "and." 或者 means "or." Your teacher corrected this and drilled 或者 with you.

4. Missing quantity word structure

You said: 两个女儿, 两车, 两只车
The classifier for vehicles is 辆, not 只 and not 个.
Correct: 两辆车
只 is for animals and some small objects. 辆 is specifically for wheeled vehicles.

You said: 如果我的女儿起床八点，我每天我也起床八点钟
More natural: 如果我女儿八点起床，我也八点起床。
The double 我 mid-sentence and the redundant 每天 break the flow. Keep the structure clean.

6. Using 好词 when you meant 好句子

Sentence Structures and Drills

This is the highest priority section. Focus on drilling these patterns out loud until they feel automatic.


1. 把 sentence structure

Pattern: Subject + 把 + Object + Verb + Result complement

The 把 sentence is the core grammar of this lesson. It emphasizes what happens to the object, and the verb must always be followed by a result (you cannot end on the verb alone).

- 他们把项目完成了。They completed the project.
- 请把菜单给我。Please give me the menu.
- 把手机放下。Put the phone down.
- 把手机打开。Turn on the phone.
- 把书给我。Give me the book.
- 小林把计划书交给了老板。Xiao Lin handed the project plan to the boss.
- 我要把这件事情安排好。I need to arrange this matter properly.
- 请你把时间安排好。Please arrange the time properly.

Drill: Generate five sentences using this pattern with objects from your own life. For example: 我每天把手机放在桌子上。我要把这个项目完成。

2. 考虑到 + situation + result

Pattern: 考虑到 + circumstances，Subject + result/assessment

- 考虑到我有工作和家庭，我的中文学习进度还不错。
- 考虑到人手不够，我们已经做得很好了。
- 考虑到预算不多，这个计划还可以。

Drill: Use this structure to describe your own situation in three different sentences.


3. 因为...所以... with 通货膨胀 or workplace topics

Pattern: 因为 + cause，(所以) + result

- 因为通货膨胀，美国人认为工资加百分之三就像没加一样。
- 因为人手不够，我们要找一个助理。
- 因为这个项目很大，小林和同事经常加班。

Note on 认为 vs 觉得: 认为 is used for opinions on objective matters (economic policy, facts, assessments). 觉得 is used for personal feelings or impressions. In a sentence about inflation and wages, 认为 is the right choice.


4. 一定 for certainty

Pattern: Subject + 一定 + verb phrase

- 如果你每天学习，你的中文一定越来越好。
- 她一定会喜欢的。
- 你的中文一定越来越好。

Note: 一定会...的 is a fixed expression for "will definitely." The 的 at the end is part of the structure and softens the certainty into a confident prediction. Do not drop it.


5. 越来越 + adjective - getting more and more

Pattern: Subject + 越来越 + adjective

- 你的中文越来越好。Your Chinese is getting better and better.
- 工资越来越低。Salaries are getting lower and lower.
- 生活越来越贵。The cost of living is getting more and more expensive.

Drill: Make three sentences using this structure about things changing in your life or work.


6. Classifiers - quick reference for this lesson

You struggled with 辆 vs 只 in the lesson. Here is the set to memorize:

- 辆 - wheeled vehicles: 一辆车，两辆车，三辆自行车
- 支 - long thin objects: 一支笔，一支蜡烛
- 只 - animals and some small objects: 一只猫，两只狗，一只手表
- 张 - flat objects (covered in April 6 session): 一张机票，一张桌子，一张照片
- 个 - general / people / abstract: 一个人，一个项目，一个计划

The most urgent one to fix is 两辆车, not 两只车 and not 两个车.


One mistake to stop making immediately

In this lesson you used 跟 incorrectly several times as a connector where it does not belong. The two usages to keep straight:

- 我跟你说 means I am speaking with you or I want to say to you (informal opening)
- 我告诉你 means I am telling you something (information follows directly)

Do not say 我告诉跟你. The two verbs do not combine this way.
