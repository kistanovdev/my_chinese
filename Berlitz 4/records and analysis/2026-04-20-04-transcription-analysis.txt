MISTAKES TO ADDRESS

1. 我做很多软件

You said 我做很多软件 to mean you work on a lot of software. 做 here is wrong in this context. You are not building or manufacturing software from scratch in the way 做 implies. The correct verb is 开发.

Correct: 我们在开发新软件。

Your teacher supplied this correction immediately. The phrase 开发软件 (to develop software) came up several times in this lesson and is the standard term.

2. 我们在工厂是 used to describe your workplace

You said 我们在工厂是 while trying to talk about your work situation. 工厂 means factory, which is for manufacturing physical goods. Software companies are not 工厂. The correct word is 公司.

Correct: 我们在公司在做新软件。

3. 苹果以后是Samsung used to mean "after Apple comes Samsung"

You used 以后 to mean "next" in a ranked list, saying 苹果以后是Samsung. This is not correct. 以后 means "in the future" or "after a point in time." For ranking sequences, use 其次 (secondly, next in order) or simply 然后.

Correct: 苹果公司的市场份额最大，其次是三星，然后是中国的手机公司。

Your teacher gave you exactly this sentence. It is the key drill sentence from this lesson.

4. 他们的广告费预算比我们有一倍，我们多一倍

This sentence came apart midway through. The correct structure for comparing quantities with 多 is:

A 比 B 多 + amount

Correct: 他们的广告费预算比我们多一倍。

Do not insert 有 between 比 and 多. Do not repeat the subject at the end.

5. 我们的分 / 收 used for market share

You started sentences with partial words and circled around. The correct full term is 市场份额, and the verb you need with it is 占.

Correct: 苹果手机市场份额占百分之二十。

The teacher taught 占 explicitly as the verb for expressing market share. This is a high-value correction.

6. 我吃饭了猪肉

You placed 了 between the verb and its object. In Chinese, 了 goes directly after the verb or at the end of the sentence, not between the verb and the object.

Correct: 我吃了猪肉。

This is the corrected form your teacher gave you and you repeated it correctly.

7. 她只二十岁 and 我的女儿很小，她只二十岁

Two problems here. First, 只 requires 有 before ages: 她只有两岁. Second, your daughter is two years old, not twenty. More importantly, your teacher pointed out that for young children, the natural word is 才, not 只有.

Correct: 我的女儿才两岁。

才 emphasizes surprise at how small or young something is. 只有 means simply "only." Use 才 when expressing that something is smaller or younger than expected.

8. 我每个月买三千美元 for mortgage payments

买 means to buy or purchase. You do not buy a mortgage payment every month. The correct verb is 还 (huán, to repay, to return).

Correct: 我每个月还三千美元的按揭贷款。

SENTENCE DRILLS

These are the four highest-priority patterns from this session. They are not repeats of previous drills.

1. Ranking with 市场份额最大，其次是...，然后是...

This is the sentence your teacher gave you as a model and repeated several times. It is the central sentence of this lesson.

Pattern: A 的市场份额最大，其次是 B，然后是 C。

- 在全球手机市场，苹果公司的市场份额最大，其次是三星，然后是中国的手机公司。
- 在饮料市场，可口可乐的市场份额最大，其次是百事可乐。
- 在电脑市场，苹果公司的市场份额不是最大，戴尔的市场份额比苹果大。

Drill: Describe the competitive landscape of any market you know using this ranking structure. Say it three times about different industries.

2. 占 + percentage for market share

Pattern: X 的市场份额占百分之 Y。

This verb 占 is the one your teacher specifically introduced in this lesson to replace the vague 分 you were using.

- 可口可乐的市场份额占百分之三十二。
- 苹果手机的市场份额占百分之二十。
- 三星的销售额占全球手机市场的百分之十八。

Drill: Say three sentences about different companies using 占百分之. Then try combining with 其次: 苹果占百分之二十，其次是三星，占百分之十八。

3. 价格涨了 and 价格跌了 with amount

Pattern: X 的价格涨了/跌了 + amount or time reference

- 猪肉的价格涨得很快。
- 这个月比上个月涨了两成。
- 苹果电脑的价格还没有跌。
- 电脑的价格一年就跌了三成。

Drill: Say four sentences using 涨 or 跌 about things you have noticed going up or down in price recently. Include at least one 越来越 sentence and one 了 sentence.

4. 虽然...，(但是)我也想...

This pattern appeared in the transcript and your teacher reinforced it. You started it correctly: 虽然我用苹果手机，我想用...

The full correct form uses 但是 or also:

Pattern: 虽然 A，(但是) B

- 虽然我用苹果手机，我也想用小米。
- 虽然中国手机很好，但是我还是用iPhone。
- 虽然苹果电脑很贵，但是很多人还是买。
- 虽然销售额很高，但是利润不高。

Drill: Make four 虽然...但是... sentences about products, technology, or work. Include at least one with 还是.
