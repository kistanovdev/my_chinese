Your main speaking bottleneck is assembling familiar words into complete sentences. The most useful practice is **word order, time expressions, and choosing a precise verb**. You already used several structures correctly: 我每天在家工作、他的腿断了、被汽车撞到了 and 对我来说.

I treat Speaker 1 as you. Without audio, pronunciation and transcription judgments remain tentative. Sentences read from lesson material show reading ability, rather than necessarily spontaneous speaking.

1. **Put time before the action.**
   
   “你喝茶每天” → **你每天喝茶。**  
   “我喝我的咖啡每天一个” → **我每天喝一杯咖啡。**
   
   Useful frame: **谁＋什么时候＋做什么**. Place also usually comes before the verb: **我每天在家工作。**

2. **Use 上课 and 开会 as complete verb phrases.**
   
   “今天我不能有中文课……四点……我要开会” → **今天下午四点我不能上中文课，因为我要开会。**
   
   **有中文课** means a class is scheduled; **上中文课** means attending it. Memorize **上午／下午＋几点**, rather than translating 上 and 下 individually. Your eventual clarification supports **下午四点**; the earlier 上午 was part of the confusion.

3. **Separate clock time from duration.**
   
   “九点……十五分钟” → **现在是九点十五分。**  
   “等五分” → **等五分钟。**  
   In the story: **五分钟后，救护人员来了。**
   
   **九点十五分** = 9:15; **十五分钟** = fifteen minutes.  
   **从九点到五点上班** gives working hours, but your transcript only establishes the nine o’clock start.

4. **Use 没 for something you haven’t done.**
   
   When asked whether you completed the exercise:  
   “我不做” → **我没做这个练习。**
   
   **不做** = don’t do it / won’t do it.  
   **没做** = didn’t do it / haven’t done it.  
   **我没看到汽车撞人。** refers to not seeing an event; **我没见过汽车撞人。** means you have never witnessed it.

5. **Put duration after the verb.**
   
   “我在美国十二年住……” → **我在美国住了十二年了。**  
   “我跟她九年认识，或一起” → choose your meaning:
   
   - **我跟她认识九年了。** — We have known each other for nine years.
   - **我们在一起九年了。** — We have been together for nine years.
   
   These do not necessarily mean nine years of marriage.

6. **Use 听懂／看懂 for understanding speech or text.**
   
   “每句我有一生词” → **每句话里都有一个生词。**  
   For the book discussion: **我大概能看懂百分之八十五。**
   
   **听懂** = understand what you hear; **看懂** = understand what you read; **明白** = understand a meaning or explanation.  
   To describe your current learning balance: **我的听力比口语好。**

7. **Build comparisons with A 比 B＋description.**
   
   Your comparison about matcha → **我觉得日本人比中国人更喜欢抹茶。**  
   “最好的城市在俄罗斯” → **对我来说，圣彼得堡是俄罗斯最好的城市。**
   
   **更** means “more”; **最** means “most.” Keeping **我觉得／对我来说** makes clear that you are expressing an opinion.

8. **Use a precise noun instead of 难看 for every negative meaning.**
   
   “难看的汽车撞” → if you meant a serious crash: **我见过一起很严重的车祸。**
   
   **难看** means ugly or unpleasant-looking; **严重** means serious.  
   “骂词／俄罗斯的骂人” → **俄语脏话／用俄语说脏话**.  
   **骂人** means insulting someone; **说脏话** means using swear words. They overlap, but are not identical.

9. **Learn questions that help you keep speaking.**
   
   “漂亮的翻译词是什么？” → if you wanted its opposite: **“漂亮”的反义词是什么？**  
   “你听过这个书？” → **你听说过这本书吗？**
   
   Useful rescue phrases:
   
   - **这个用中文怎么说？**
   - **我忘了这个词怎么说。**
   - **我的意思是……**
   
   These are more useful for spontaneous speech than memorizing long specialist titles.

10. **Distinguish quantity, frequency, speed, and cost.**
    
    **每天喝一次咖啡** describes frequency; **每天喝一杯咖啡** describes quantity. Your earlier coffee quantity is unclear, so don’t automatically turn “两次” into “两杯.”
    
    “开车一百二十公斤” → **车速是每小时一百二十公里。**  
    **公斤** = kilograms; **公里** = kilometers; speed needs **每小时**.
    
    “你觉得七百美元高？” → **你觉得这台咖啡机贵吗？**  
    **东西贵／便宜；价格高／低。**  
    For your annual coffee calculation: **一年大概要花两千美元。**
