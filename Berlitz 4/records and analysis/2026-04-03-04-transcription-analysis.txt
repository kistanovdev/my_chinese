Mistakes to Address

1. Using 二 instead of 两 before measure words

You said 她是二岁 and 二年 several times before self-correcting.
The rule is: when counting before a measure word or noun, use 两, not 二.
Correct: 她两岁，两个月，两个星期，两岁零七个月。
二 is for ordinal use or when reading numbers in sequence (第二，二十，电话号码). When you put a number directly before a noun or measure word, it is always 两.

2. Saying 第一东西 and 第二东西

You said 第一东西，第二个东西 when trying to describe doing one thing before another.
Your teacher corrected this twice: the word for tasks or actions is 事情, not 东西.
东西 is a physical object or stuff. 事情 is a matter, task, or thing you do.
Correct: 第一件事情，第二件事情. Or simply use the 先...再 structure directly without labeling them.

3. Incomplete 先...再 attempts

You understood the concept but initially tried to describe it as 可以说他一定做两，两个东西.
The actual pattern is structural, not descriptive. Do not explain the pattern in the sentence itself. Just use it:
先洗手，再吃饭. That is the complete sentence.

4. Using 跟我 as a filler at the start of sentences

You used 跟我 repeatedly at the start of sentences where it did not belong, for example 跟我，我的女儿... or 跟我，在美国.... This is a habit to eliminate. 跟我 means with me or together with me. It is not a sentence starter or discourse filler. Start your sentences directly: 在美国... 我的女儿...

5. Word order with 工作 as a verb phrase

You said 工作在周末不好.
More natural: 在周末加班不好 or 周末工作不好.
Place the time expression at the start or directly after the subject, not after the verb.

6. Sentence structure for 满意

You said 我觉得学习中文是满意.
Your teacher corrected this: 满意 uses the structure 对 + topic + 满意 or 很满意.
Correct: 我对我的中文学习很满意.
Do not use 是满意. Do not place 满意 at the end of a 觉得 sentence without restructuring.

Sentence Structures and Drills

These are the highest priority patterns from this lesson. Practice them out loud until the structure comes automatically.


1. 先...再... for sequential actions

Pattern: 先 + action 1，再 + action 2
Meaning: first do X, then do Y. The order is fixed and intentional.

- 妈妈要小明先洗手，再吃饭。
- 王阿姨先去超市买菜，再到学校接孩子。
- 我先刷牙，再睡觉。
- 我们先去超市，再去接孩子。

Drill: Describe your own morning or evening routine using 先...再 for at least four actions.


2. 才 for a condition that must first be met

Pattern: condition + 才 + result
Meaning: only when the condition is true does the result apply. The result cannot happen without the condition.

- 五岁才可以去幼儿园。Only at five can you go to kindergarten.
- 因为小刘跳槽了，人手不够，所以拖了几天才完成。It was only after a few days of delay that it was finally completed.
- 来不及了，赶不上飞机了。There is no time, cannot catch the flight.
- 我学了一年中文，才开始看懂一些汉字。Only after a year of studying Chinese did I begin to understand some characters.

Drill: Make three sentences using 才 describing something in your life that requires a condition before it can happen.


3. 提前 / 按时 / 推迟 three-way contrast

This is a core vocabulary cluster from this lesson. Practice all three in the same context so the contrast is clear.

- 项目计划四月三十号完成。按时完成就是四月三十号完成。提前完成就是四月二十四号完成。推迟就是五月十号才完成。
- 他们提前一个星期完成了，客户一定会很高兴的。
- 因为小刘跳槽了，人手不够，项目推迟了一个星期。
- 老陈今天要加班，不能按时下班。

Drill: Take one project or task from your own work and create three sentences, one for each scenario: 提前完成，按时完成，推迟了。


4. 来得及 vs 来不及

Pattern: Subject + 来得及 / 来不及 + verb phrase (optional)
Meaning: 来得及 = there is enough time, will make it. 来不及 = no time, will not make it.

- 现在九点半出门，十点到公司，来得及。
- 现在十点出门，十点到公司，来不及，会迟到。
- 我要坐八点的飞机去北京，来得及吗？
- 来不及了，赶不上飞机了，没时间了。
- 不好意思，今天来不及和你吃饭了。

Drill: Describe a scenario where you are rushing somewhere, using 来得及 or 来不及 naturally in context.


5. 对...满意 / 不满意

Pattern: Subject + 对 + topic + (很/不) 满意
Meaning: to be (un)satisfied with something

- 我对我的中文学习很满意。
- 我对我现在的工资满意，但是我对我以前的工资不满意。
- 很多美国人对他们的工作不满意。
- 客户对我们很满意。

Drill: Use this structure to make four sentences about things in your life you are satisfied or unsatisfied with. Try using 但是 to connect a positive and negative in the same sentence.


6. 再也不...了 for emphatic never again

Pattern: Subject + 再也不 + verb + 了
Meaning: will absolutely never do something again. Emphatic and final.

- 那个饭店的菜又贵又不好吃，我再也不去了。
- 法国迪士尼的饭馆很不好，再也不去了。
- 如果他再也不来上班，我们就要解雇他。

Drill: Think of something you have experienced that was disappointing and form a sentence using 再也不...了.
