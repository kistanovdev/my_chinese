### Ten high-value corrections and structures

1. **Put location before the action**  
   “工作在家” → **在家工作**  
   “我在沙加缅度……住在” → **我住在沙加缅度。**  
   Pattern: **谁 + 在哪里 + 做什么**; for residence, **谁 + 住在 + 地方**.  
   Useful extension: **我每个月去公司两次。开车去公司要两个小时。**

2. **Use 回 for returning; 住 for staying overnight**  
   “开车去我的家” → **开车回家。**  
   “我在 Cupertino 睡觉” → **我在 Cupertino 住一晚。** if you mean staying overnight.  
   睡觉 means “sleep”; 住一晚 means “stay one night.” Your exact travel days and departure time remain unclear.

3. **Express liking both options with 都 / 也**  
   “我喜欢一样，跟我一样” → **两个我都喜欢。**  
   “我都喜欢去公司，也……工作在家” → **我喜欢去公司，也喜欢在家工作。**  
   If you mean *equally*: **去公司和在家工作，我一样喜欢。**

4. **Learn whole verb phrases: 开会、说话、修车**  
   “跟同事有会” → **跟同事开会。**  
   “我想说……人” → **我想跟别人说话。**  
   “做修” → **修车。**  
   **跟 + 人 + 动作** is especially useful: 跟同事开会、跟朋友聊天.  
   For requesting vocabulary: **“sometimes”用中文怎么说？**

5. **Distinguish permission, necessity and restriction**  
   **我可以在家工作。** = I’m allowed to work from home.  
   **我只能在家工作。** = I can *only* work from home.  
   Your “我只能在家工作” likely changed your intended meaning, given that you also described office visits.  
   “你一定去公司” → **你一定要去公司。 / 你必须去公司。** if attendance is required.

6. **Separate 不, 没 and 过**  
   “我的车不坏了 / 没坏了” → **我的车没坏。** if it wasn’t damaged.  
   “2018年我不看中国的车” → **2018年，我没看到中国的车。**  
   - **不**: habits, preferences or refusal — 我不骑摩托车。  
   - **没**: something didn’t happen — 我没看到。  
   - **过**: prior experience — 我看过。 / 我没看过。  
   
   **没看到这个字** means you didn’t see it on that occasion; **没见过这个字** means it is unfamiliar.

7. **Use 有点 + adjective; verb + 一点**  
   “一点快” → **有点快。**  
   “痛一点” → **有点痛。** if you mean “it hurts a little.”  
   “唱歌一点” → **唱一点歌。**  
   有点 describes a degree, often something undesirable. 一点 after a verb can indicate a small amount; **慢一点** means “a little slower.”

8. **Describe how someone acts with 得**  
   “加州人不……开车很好” → **我觉得加州有些人开车开得不太好。**  
   Pattern: **动作 + 得 + 描述** — 开得快、说得慢、做得好.  
   Also: **开汽车，骑自行车，骑摩托车。**  
   You already used 越来越多 correctly; extend it to **车做得越来越好了。**

9. **Keep the action in a 被 sentence**  
   “过马路的老太太被自行车了” → **过马路的老太太被自行车撞了。**  
   Pattern: **受到影响的人/东西 + 被 + 动作的执行者 + 动作**.  
   Compare: **一辆车撞了我的车。 → 我的车被另一辆车撞了。**  
   The missing 撞 could be a transcription omission; later you produced **老人被自行车撞了** correctly.

10. **Use age and duration chunks; preserve uncertainty**  
    “四年、五年” → **四岁、五岁。** for age.  
    “我在医院……一个星期住在” → **我在医院住了一个星期。**  
    **我不记得了。** = I don’t remember.  
    **我记不清了，可能住了几个星期。** = I don’t remember clearly; perhaps a few weeks.  
    Here **我应该住院** can sound like “I should be hospitalized.” Use 可能 when recalling uncertain facts. When asked whether the engineer resells cars, **我不知道** fits better than **我没有意见** (“I have no objection/opinion”).
