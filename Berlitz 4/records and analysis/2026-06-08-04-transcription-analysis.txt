### Ten priorities

1. **住在 + place: finish the location phrase**
   
   You repeatedly started with **“我在……住在……”** (07:28, 14:07, 24:46).
   
   → **我住在郊区。**  
   → **我住在加州。**
   
   For ordinary actions, use **person + time + 在 + place + action**: **我今天在家工作。** With **住在**, keep the residence after it. **郊区** is usually the clearer word for residential suburbs; **郊外** suggests the outskirts or countryside.

2. **岁 for age; 年 for duration**
   
   **“大女儿有三年”“小女儿有一年”** (21:23–22:09)
   
   → **我的大女儿三岁，小女儿一岁。**
   
   **“我从十六岁……时候住在美国”** (19:06)
   
   → **我从十六岁起就住在美国。**
   
   **三年以后** means “three years later”; **三岁的时候** means “when someone is three.”

3. **还不会 + skill; 还没 + event**
   
   You alternated between **“不说”“还没说”“不能说”** while describing your daughters.
   
   → **小女儿还不会说话。** — She cannot talk yet.  
   → **大女儿还没说过“我想养一只狗”。** — She has not said this yet.
   
   **不会** concerns ability; **没说过** concerns something that has not happened. **不能** often means circumstances or permission prevent it.

4. **想 + verb, and useful measure words**
   
   **“我想一个狗”“我小的时候想一只狗”** (21:43, 23:05)
   
   → **我想养一只狗。**  
   → **我小时候想养一只狗。**
   
   Learn these as chunks: **一只狗、一套公寓、一首歌**. For **“我买了我的家”** (26:00), say **我买了一套房子。** **家** means home/family; **房子** is the property you buy.

5. **比 for comparisons; 跟……不一样 for differences**
   
   Your comparison about suburbs needed a finished predicate (29:01):
   
   → **我觉得美国的郊区比市中心好。**
   
   **“音乐……比……不一样”** (32:56)
   
   → **我觉得西方音乐跟中国音乐很不一样。**
   
   Use **A 比 B + adjective** to compare a quality, and **A 跟 B 不一样** to say they differ.

6. **Negating adjectives; 有点 versus 一点**
   
   **“没有热”** (41:38)
   
   → **今天不热。**
   
   **“一点方便”** (42:36)
   
   → **交通还算方便。** — Transportation is reasonably convenient.
   
   **有点 + adjective** often introduces an undesirable quality: **房租有点贵。**  
   **Adjective + 一点** means “a little more…”: **能不能便宜一点？**

7. **有 for existence; adjectives for convenience**
   
   **“我没有地铁”** (15:02) sounds as though you personally lack a subway.
   
   → **我住的地方没有地铁。**
   
   **“如果你有你的车……有很好的交通”** (25:27)
   
   → **如果你有车，出门就很方便。**
   
   **“只我们可以”** (26:51)
   
   → **只有我们小区的住户可以进去。**
   
   This describes access without guessing which facility you meant.

8. **过 for experience; 是……的 for a past detail**
   
   **“房子有新装修吗？”** (58:07)
   
   → **这套房子装修过吗？** — Has it been renovated?  
   → **这套房子是什么时候装修的？** — When was it renovated?
   
   Your **“我2024年去过日本”** is grammatical. For a direct answer about the year, **我是2024年去日本的** is especially natural.
   
   Replace **“我去过日本，也……和韩国”** with either **我去过日本和韩国** or **我去过日本，也去过韩国**.

9. **搬进来／搬进去: choose a viewpoint**
   
   Your **“搬进去”** (1:10:23) is possible when viewing the house from outside. **搬进来** fits someone speaking from, or adopting the viewpoint of, the house.
   
   Useful chunks: **搬家** = move home; **搬出去** = move out; **租出去** = rent out.
   
   The lesson’s **把 + object + action + result** is worth practicing: **我把东西整理好了。** — I have put everything in order.

10. **要／得 for practical obligations**
    
    **“老师，我一定出去，我有一个开会”** (1:18:42)
    
    → **老师，我得先走了，我要开会。**
    
    **开会** is a verb: “attend/hold a meeting.” Alternatively: **我有一个会议。** **一定** expresses certainty or determination; **得** (*děi*) expresses necessity.
