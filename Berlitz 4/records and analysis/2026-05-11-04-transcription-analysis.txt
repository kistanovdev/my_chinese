### Ten useful corrections and structures

1. **Describe weather directly; put 大概 before the number.**  
   “天气是晴天” is understandable. More natural: **今天是晴天。／今天天气很好。**  
   Instead of “不知道大概”: **我不知道多少度。／今天大概二十五度。**  
   大概 = approximately; 差不多 = about / almost the same. Usually one is enough.  
   Also, °C is **摄氏度**, and °F is **华氏度**. The teacher’s explanation that C is simply called 度 was incomplete.

2. **看得到／看不到 — whether something is visible.**  
   During the video problem, “我看你” means “I look at you.” For “I can see you,” say:  
   **我看得到你。／现在能看到你了。**  
   Your “我不知道你可以看” becomes: **我不知道你能不能看到。**  
   **看到了** emphasizes that you have seen something; **看得到** emphasizes visibility.

3. **Use a complete question when asking for language help.**  
   “怎么词是 table？” → **哪个词是 table 的意思？**  
   Useful phrases:
   - **……用中文怎么说？** — How do you say … in Chinese?
   - **我应该用“事情”还是“东西”？** — Should I use 事情 or 东西?
   - **我不知道这个词，但我能根据上下文猜出意思。** — I can guess from context.  
   
   东西 usually refers to things or objects; 事情 refers to matters or events.

4. **Use 会 for an expected reaction; 希望 for a wish.**  
   You repeatedly said “我觉得你喜欢” when recommending videos. More natural:  
   **我觉得你会喜欢这个视频。** — I think you’ll like this video.  
   “我想明天你很好” → **我希望你明天就好起来。**  
   **我觉得……** expresses an opinion; **对我来说……** means “for me,” especially for personal experience or preference.

5. **……的时候 + frequency or age.**  
   “我小的时候做饭很多” → **我小时候经常做饭。**  
   “孩子时候” → **小时候**  
   “二十年的时候” → **二十岁的时候**, if you mean age twenty.  
   Your travel story can begin: **我跟奶奶去纽约旅游的时候，……**  
   年 counts years; 岁 expresses age. For “when I was older,” use **长大以后** or **大一点的时候**.

6. **Verb + 得 + description.**  
   “我不睡觉很好” likely meant poor sleep: **我昨晚睡得不好。**  
   不睡觉 means “not sleep,” rather than “sleep badly.”  
   Other useful corrections:
   - “第二天我们走很多” → **第二天我们走了很多路。**
   - “我们不住在医院很多” → **我们很少住院。**, if you mean infrequently; **我们住院的时间不长。**, if you mean short stays.

7. **没（有） for something that did not happen; adjective predicates need no 是.**  
   “她不……骨折了” → **她没有骨折，但是身体不太舒服。**  
   **我的腿受伤了。／我的腿很疼。**  
   Useful distinctions: **受伤** = get injured; **伤口** = wound; **伤疤** = scar.  
   不舒服 means unwell or uncomfortable and can include pain; it does not necessarily exclude 疼.

8. **Result words make accident descriptions precise.**  
   “我滑倒了很多次” was already correct. Keep these distinctions:
   - **滑倒** — slip and fall
   - **绊倒** — trip and fall
   - **摔倒／摔了一跤** — fall / take a fall
   - **切到手** — accidentally cut your hand
   - **烫伤** — get scalded or burned by heat  
   
   A useful complete sentence: **切菜的时候，我不小心切到手了。**  
   不小心 = accidentally / carelessly.

9. **把／被 + action + result; add direction when needed.**  
   You correctly read: **他被石头绊倒了。**  
   Compare:
   - **石头把他绊倒了。**
   - **我不小心把手烫伤了。**
   - **我帮你把东西拿上去吧。** — Let me help carry the things upstairs.
   
   把 and 被 change the sentence’s perspective; they are not interchangeable words.  
   “他能走路回家” is understandable; **他能自己走回家。** is more compact.

10. **比…… + adjective; 越来越……; ……才……**  
   These are especially useful beyond this lesson:
   - **今天比昨天好一点。** — A little better than yesterday.
   - “越来越好明白” → **我越来越明白了。**
   - **要休息两个月，才能上班。** — Only after resting two months can the person return to work.
   
   Treat the recovery sentence as a grammar example, not a general medical rule.  
   **好好休息** means “rest well”; **只好休息** means “have no choice but to rest.”
