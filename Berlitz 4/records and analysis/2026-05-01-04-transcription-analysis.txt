Mistakes to Address

离婚 vs 结婚 vs 婚礼
This was your most persistent error of the session. You said 从她的离婚, 参加离婚, and 我离婚的时候, all when you meant the opposite. 离婚 is divorce. The words you needed were:

婚礼 (the wedding ceremony, the event)
结婚 (the verb, to get married)
参加婚礼 (to attend a wedding)
我结婚的时候 (when I got married)
参加她的婚礼以前 (before attending her wedding)
Your teacher corrected this multiple times. Drill the contrast 结婚 / 离婚 / 婚礼 until it is automatic.

一张礼服 - wrong measure word for clothing
You said 买一张礼服. 张 is for flat things like paper, photos, and tables. For clothing use 件 for a single piece and 套 for a full matched set.

一件礼服 (one piece of formal wear)
一套礼服 (a full suit with jacket, trousers, and tie)
以后 used where 以前 is needed
You said 以后，俄罗斯也是共产主义 when you were describing the past. 以后 means after or in the future. 以前 means before or in the past. The same error appeared again when describing events before the wedding.

以前，俄罗斯也是共产主义
参加她的婚礼以前，我没穿过这个颜色的礼服
我的电脑总是充电了
总是 expresses a habit. 了 marks a completed action. The two do not go together. The meaning you wanted was that your computer always needs to stay plugged in.

Your teacher corrected this to: 你的电脑总是需要插线

When expressing a habitual necessity, use 总是需要 + verb, not 总是 + verb + 了.

免费 vs 零钱
You said 它是零钱 when trying to say it was free. 零钱 means small coins or loose change. The correct word is 免费 (free of charge). Your teacher modeled the full sentence: 我现在的苹果显示器是公司免费给我的.

Sentence Drills

怎么办 for situations where you do not know what to do
Pattern: situation + 怎么办. It always goes at the end. Then practice pairing it with 那就...吧 to propose a solution.

没有礼服，怎么办？那就去租一件吧。
上班迟到了，怎么办？那就快打电话给老板吧。
女儿哭了，怎么办？
钱包丢了，怎么办？
Drill: Think of three real situations that stress you out. Build a 怎么办 sentence, then answer with 那就...吧.

带 + person + 去 + place
Pattern: 带 + person + 去 + place + (activity verb)
Meaning: to take someone along somewhere

我经常带女儿去公园玩。
他带我去那家小店租礼服。
你能带我去吗？
我带太太去看电影。
Drill: Four sentences using 带 with different people and destinations each week.

event + 以前 / event + 以后
The reference point comes first, then 以前 or 以后. This is the reverse of English word order.

结婚以前，我没穿过礼服。
参加婚礼以前，你应该提前准备好。
下班以后，我带女儿去公园玩。
来美国以前，我从来没喝过这种酒。
Drill: Four sentences, two with 以前 and two with 以后, about events in your own life. Pay careful attention to which one you reach for - 以前 and 以后 are still getting swapped.

adjective + 极了
Meaning: absolutely, to the extreme. More expressive than 非常 in casual spoken reactions.

好听极了
精彩极了
好看极了
热闹极了
Drill: React to five things you have experienced this week using adjective + 极了. Food, music, a film, a place, or a person's performance all work.

是...免费给我的 for something received at no cost
这台显示器是公司免费给我的。
这件礼服是朋友免费给我的。
这本书是老师免费给我们的。
Drill: Think of three things in your life you received without paying. Build one sentence for each.
