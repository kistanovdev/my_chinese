Mistakes to Address

我从洛杉矶来了
You said: 我从啊洛杉矶来了
The teacher corrected this to the more natural: 昨天我在洛杉矶听了演唱会，今天我开车从洛杉矶回来了。
The issue is two-fold. First, 来了 alone is fine for arriving, but the sentence needs the event context to be meaningful. Second, 回来 is the correct verb compound when returning from a place. Plain 来了 works but implies you came here, not that you returned from somewhere. When the idea is returning, use 回来.
Correct: 今天我开车从洛杉矶回来了。

从星期一去星期五
You said: 从星期一去星期五
Your teacher immediately corrected this: 从...到..., not 从...去...
去 means to go somewhere. It cannot connect two time points.
Correct: 从星期一到星期五

肯定 used in the negative
You said: 我不肯定上中文课
Your teacher explained this is not natural. 肯定 functions as an affirmative adverb. To express uncertainty, use 不一定.
Correct: 我不一定上中文课
You can say 我肯定上中文课 and 我不一定上中文课, but not 我不肯定上中文课.

我们有四问题
You said: 我们有四问题
Correct: 我们有四个问题
The measure word 个 is required before 问题. You cannot place a bare number directly before a noun in Chinese the way you can in English.

小刘肯定在出来
You said: 小刘肯定在出来
Your teacher corrected: 小刘可能在出差
Two errors here. First, 出来 means to come out. The word you wanted is 出差, meaning on a business trip. Second, based on the context (she might be on a trip, but it is not confirmed), 可能 is more appropriate than 肯定.

肯定做去中国旅游吗
You said: 肯定做去中国旅游吗
做 does not belong here. 肯定 already functions as the modal adverb. The verb is 去.
Correct: 我明年肯定去中国旅游。

我还没有中国旅游过
You said: 我还没有中国旅游过
Correct: 我还没有在中国旅游过
The location needs 在 to mark it as a place of action, not a direct object. 旅游 does not take a place as its direct object.

我们的车充电两次 without a main verb
You said: 我们一定我们的车充电两次
The teacher modeled the correct version: 我们肯定需要给我们的车充电两次 / 我们不得不给我们的车充电两次.
给 is required here to indicate "charging the car" as a beneficiary action. The structure is 给 + object + verb, not simply object + verb.

Sentence Drills

These are the highest priority structures. Practice each one out loud until it is automatic.

肯定 / 一定 / 可能 / 不一定 / 不可能 five-way contrast
This is the core grammar block of the lesson. The key is knowing which word fits which degree of certainty. Drill all five using the same topic.

他每天都上班，所以今天他肯定在公司。
他一定在公司。(same strong certainty, slightly more emphatic)
周末他可能在家休息，也可能出去玩。
他周末不一定在家，也许出去旅游了。
周末他不可能在公司，公司不开门。
Make your own set of five sentences about one person in your life using all five words. Then do another set about travel plans.

从...到... for time and distance
Pattern: 从 + start point + 到 + end point
This works for both time and physical distance.

从星期一到星期五，我每天在家工作。
从我家到洛杉矶，大概六个小时。
从北京到上海，坐高铁两个多小时。
从早上九点到下午六点，我在公司上班。
Drill: Make four sentences using 从...到..., two with time expressions and two with locations.

不得不for no choice but to
Pattern: subject + 不得不 + verb phrase
Meaning: cannot avoid it, have no choice

我们不得不给我们的车充电两次。
因为下雨，我不得不待在家里。
因为堵车，我不得不坐地铁。
因为项目推迟了，我们不得不加班。
Drill: Think of three real situations in your life where you had no choice. Make one 不得不 sentence for each.

还没有...过 for never having done something
Pattern: subject + 还没有 + verb + 过
Or: subject + 还没有 + verb + 过 + object (the 过 can come before or after the object)

我还没有在中国旅游过。
我还没有中过奖。
我还没有去过新疆。
我还没有吃过粤菜。
Note: 还 implies you expect or want to do it eventually. If you simply have never done something with no expectation, use 从来没有...过.

Drill: Make four sentences about things you have never done but would like to. Use 还没有...过 for each.

对我来说 for stating your personal perspective
Pattern: 对我来说 + opinion sentence
Meaning: from my point of view, as far as I am concerned

对我来说，地理很有意思。
对我来说，去中国出差很辛苦。
对我来说，学中文不容易，但是很有意思。
对我来说，演唱会比电影更有意思。
Drill: Make five sentences starting with 对我来说 about topics from today's lesson: Chinese study, traveling, work trips, concerts, geography.

One item to fix from this session specifically

You still said 我不肯定 twice after your teacher corrected it. The negative form of 肯定 used as uncertainty is 不一定, not 不肯定. Make a note of this distinction now. 不肯定 sounds like you are un-confirming something official. 不一定 is the natural expression of not being sure. Swap it every time.
