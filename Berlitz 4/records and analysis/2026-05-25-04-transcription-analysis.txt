Your main speaking bottleneck is choosing the right verb and assembling familiar words quickly. The most useful practice is with short sentence frames for past activities, paying, helping, travel, and time.

I treat Speaker 1 as you. Without audio, transcription errors remain uncertain; pauses and self-corrections are not automatically mistakes.

### Ten priorities

1. **了 versus 过: a specific event versus experience**
   
   You repeatedly used **我们去过** when describing the weekend. For that particular outing:
   
   **周末我们去爬山了，走了五英里。**  
   *We went hiking this weekend and walked five miles.*
   
   For past experience, **过** fits:
   
   **我以前爬山的时候走过二十英里。**
   
   Your later **我去过一次** was correct. **去了爬山** is understandable; **去爬山了** is a smoother default.

2. **走路 + distance or duration**
   
   **大女儿去一点** → **大女儿只走了一小段路。**  
   **不喜欢走很多** → **不喜欢走太远。**
   
   If you meant ten minutes:
   
   **她走了十分钟以后，就说：“你背我吧。”**
   
   Say **五英里、三公里**, without **个**. **背（bēi）** means carry on your back; **抱** means hold in your arms.

3. **帮 + person + action**
   
   **帮忙我的太太** → **我帮太太打扫家里。**
   
   Useful frame: **帮谁做什么** — help someone do something.
   
   **洗我们的家** → **打扫家里**.  
   **洗澡** means bathe or shower; **洗衣服** means wash clothes.

4. **买、付、交、还: choose the verb for the expense**
   
   You repeatedly used **买** for expenses and services:
   
   - **买账单** → **付账单**：**我在网上付账单。**
   - **买水电费** → **交水电费**：**我每个月交水电费。**
   - **每月买房子** → **每个月还房贷。**
   - **买女儿的中文课** → **给女儿报中文课。**
   
   **买** purchases something; **付／交** pays; **还（huán）** repays a loan. **给女儿买东西** is correct when you mean buying things for her.

5. **Say what the percentage measures**
   
   **我的家是百分之十五** leaves the relationship unclear.
   
   **我每个月用工资的百分之十五还房贷。**  
   *I use 15% of my salary each month to repay the mortgage.*
   
   Similarly: **我用工资的百分之二交水电费。**
   
   A more compact structure to recognize: **水电费占工资的百分之二。** — Utilities account for 2% of salary.

6. **以前／以后；上次／下次**
   
   This was a recurring mix-up:
   
   **四年以前** is grammatical; **四年前** is more concise.  
   **认识我以前，她用她的名字是她的密码** →  
   **认识我以前，她用自己的名字当密码。**
   
   **上次** = last time; **下次** = next time. The teacher’s “last night = 上次” was incorrect: last night is **昨晚**.

7. **如果……就…… + 办护照**
   
   **做护照** → **办护照**; more formally, **办理护照**.
   
   For the plan you described:
   
   **如果今年能给小女儿办好护照，我们就去中国旅游；如果办不好，就明年去。**
   
   **办好** means successfully finish arranging it. **就** makes the connection between the condition and result clear.

8. **从……坐……去……**
   
   **我想去高铁** → **我想坐高铁。**
   
   **我想从北京坐高铁去上海。**
   
   Frame: **从 + starting place + 坐 + transport + 去 + destination**.
   
   Your **从北京到上海坐高铁** was understandable; the model above is easier to reuse. Also: **去中国旅游**, rather than **旅游中国**.

9. **比 + adjective; 越来越 + adjective**
   
   **一个护照比中国的签证很便宜** →  
   **办护照比办签证便宜。**
   
   This corrects the grammar of your comparison; it does not verify the prices. In a basic **比** sentence, omit **很**:
   
   **A比B便宜。** — A is cheaper than B.  
   **A比B便宜很多。** — A is much cheaper than B.
   
   Keep the lesson’s useful change pattern: **生活费越来越高了。／汽油越来越贵了。**

10. **很 + adjective; 能力 + 得／不 + result**
    
    **有方便，有安全** → **很方便，也很安全。**
    
    These meanings need separating:
    
    - **方便** — convenient
    - **好用／容易用** — easy to use
    - **安全** — safe or secure
    
    For reading, **不能读** can suggest prohibition. If you mean comprehension:
    
    **这本书我看不懂。**  
    **第一个字我不认识。**
    
    You also correctly produced useful frames such as **我觉得……**, **她喜欢的运动**, and **俄文和西班牙文都很好**.
